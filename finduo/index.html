🌍 How global sharing works

Right now FinDuo is 100% offline — your XP, streak and league live in this browser, and the other learners are friendly simulated players. It works instantly, no signup.

To share with real people worldwide, FinDuo adds a small backend: you sign in, finished lessons send your weekly XP to a server, and it returns the real global leaderboard + a public profile for your share card.

All progress runs through one Store object, so swapping localStorage for an API (POST /xp, GET /leaderboard) is a small change. This repo already uses FastAPI + PostgreSQL.
