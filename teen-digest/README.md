# The Curious Giraffe 🦒 — Daily Growth Digest

A daily 3-minute email digest for a curious 11–15 year old reader
(hangrygiraffes@gmail.com), curated and sent automatically every morning at
7:00 AM Eastern from the parent's Gmail account.

## Goal
Build critical thinking and real-world fluency — books, teen-relevant news,
finance (all the way to hedge funds, private equity, venture capital),
AI, and the creator economy — gently, one small daily dose at a time
(润物细无声). In ~6 months the reader should be able to self-drive her own
learning about investing, startups, and turning her own interests
(anime and beyond) into long-term projects.

## How it works
- A scheduled Routine fires daily, spawns a fresh session, and follows
  `DIGEST_GUIDE.md` (format & rules) + `CURRICULUM.md` (the 26-week arc).
- The week number is computed from the anchor date **2026-08-24** (Week 1
  starts that Monday), so no state needs to be stored between runs.
- Content is searched fresh each day (weekly hot books, real news, working
  links). The email is short, link-rich, and ends with a question — never a
  lecture.

## Files
- `CURRICULUM.md` — the 26-week (6-month) topic arc, Phase 1–6.
- `DIGEST_GUIDE.md` — daily email structure, weekday themes, tone rules,
  quality bar, and safety rules.

## Changing things
Edit these files on branch `claude/teen-reading-finance-literacy-sx3xig` and
push — the next morning's digest picks the changes up automatically.
