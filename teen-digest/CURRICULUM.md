# The Curious Giraffe — 26-Week Curriculum Arc

Week 1 begins Monday **2026-08-24** (America/New_York). Compute the current
week as: `week = floor((today - 2026-08-24) / 7 days) + 1`. After Week 26,
loop back through the phases at greater depth (pick a fresh angle on the
same phase topics; never repeat an old example).

Each week has ONE core idea. Every weekday email connects its theme to that
week's core idea when it can do so naturally — never force it.

## Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4 · Money Is a Language
- **W1 — Compound interest & time.** The doubling penny, Rule of 72. Time
  beats amount.
- **W2 — What money actually is.** Barter → gold → paper → digital. Banks,
  inflation ("why did my boba get more expensive?").
- **W3 — Choices & opportunity cost.** Budgets, saving vs. spending, "every
  yes is a no to something else."
- **W4 — How a company works.** Revenue, costs, profit, business models —
  taught through the anime industry ($33B/yr: streaming, merch, production
  committees).

## Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8 · How Markets Work
- **W5 — Stocks = owning a slice.** What a share is, why prices move.
- **W6 — Bonds & interest rates.** Lending, why rates move everything.
- **W7 — Index funds & diversification.** Don't put all eggs in one basket;
  why boring often wins.
- **W8 — Bubbles & crashes.** Tulip mania, 1929, dot-com. How hype works —
  core critical-thinking week.

## Phase 3 · Weeks 9–13 · Meet the Pros
- **W9 — Hedge funds.** What they actually do; long/short; who invests in
  them.
- **W10 — Short selling & the GameStop saga.** Betting against, squeezes,
  Reddit vs. Wall Street.
- **W11 — Private equity.** Buying whole companies, fixing (or stripping)
  them; debate both views.
- **W12 — Venture capital & startups.** Why VCs fund 10 failures for 1 win;
  what a pitch is.
- **W13 — Great investors & their rules.** Buffett/Munger, value vs. growth,
  "circle of competence."

## Phase 4 · Weeks 14–17 · Mind Games (Behavioral)
- **W14 — Brain bugs.** FOMO, loss aversion, herd behavior — in markets AND
  in school life.
- **W15 — Probability & risk.** Expected value, why lotteries and gacha
  pulls are designed the way they are.
- **W16 — Scam radar.** Too-good-to-be-true, influencer "finfluencers,"
  how to verify a claim.
- **W17 — Incentives & negotiation.** "Show me the incentive and I'll show
  you the outcome"; negotiating in real life.

## Phase 5 · Weeks 18–22 · AI & the Future Economy
- **W18 — How AI actually works.** Training, data, why it makes mistakes.
- **W19 — Who makes money in AI.** Chips → models → apps; the "picks and
  shovels" idea.
- **W20 — AI × art × anime.** The great debate: tool or threat? Both sides,
  she judges.
- **W21 — Jobs of the future.** What machines are bad at; skills that
  compound.
- **W22 — Building with AI.** Kids/teens who shipped real things with AI
  tools; what she could build.

## Phase 6 · Weeks 23–26 · Builder Mode (Passion → Project)
- **W23 — The creator economy & IP.** Who owns a character? Fan art,
  copyright, licensing, why IP is the anime industry's real gold.
- **W24 — Hobby → project.** Real teens who turned fandom into zines,
  shops, channels, cons. Small experiments beat big plans.
- **W25 — Pitching & storytelling.** How VCs hear stories; how to pitch an
  idea in 60 seconds.
- **W26 — Capstone.** Recap the 6 months; invite her to draft her OWN
  one-page idea (any interest, anime or not) and reply with it.

## Standing threads (woven through all phases)
- **Books:** every Monday, 2–3 picks from what is genuinely hot that week
  (one novel, one non-fiction, one graphic novel/manga), age 11–15.
- **Critical thinking:** every email ends with one open question with no
  single right answer.
- **Agency:** links are invitations to explore, never homework.
