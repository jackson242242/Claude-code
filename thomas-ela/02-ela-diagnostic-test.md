## 📣 MISSION 4 — Make Your Case (Campaign HQ)

**Your role:** You're running a **campaign**. The principal will read the most convincing
argument and actually decide. Your job: read **both fact sheets**, then write a pitch that
uses evidence from **both** to win. (This is the big one — take your time and plan first.)

> **FACT SHEET A — From the school nurse:**
> Studies show that kids aged 9–12 need 9 to 11 hours of sleep, but most get far less.
> Sleep scientists have found that the brain of a young person doesn't naturally feel sleepy
> until later at night, so waking at 6 a.m. for a 7:45 start means many students arrive
> exhausted. Tired students struggle to focus, remember less, and get sick more often.
>
> **FACT SHEET B — From the transportation office:**
> The school's buses are shared with the high school, which starts at 7:15. If the elementary
> start time moves later, the district would need extra buses, costing roughly $90,000 a year.
> A later start would also push dismissal to 4:15, overlapping with many parents' work hours
> and shortening time for after-school sports and clubs.
