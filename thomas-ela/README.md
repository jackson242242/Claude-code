## Why this exists

The NWEA MAP **Family Report** only reports an *overall* Reading score (RIT 218,
82nd percentile). It does **not** break that score into sub-skills, so it can tell
you Thomas is a strong reader — but not *which* parts of English Language Arts to
work on next. This package fills that gap.

It contains four connected pieces, in the order you use them:

| # | File | What it is | When to use |
|---|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | [`01-diagnosis-weighted.md`](01-diagnosis-weighted.md) | **Weighted diagnosis** of Thomas's ELA profile — every skill domain scored for importance *and* current confidence, combined into a priority ranking. | Read first. It explains the "why" behind everything else. |
| 2 | [`02-ela-diagnostic-test.md`](02-ela-diagnostic-test.md) | The **ELA test** — a **performance-task "Mission Pack"** (not a worksheet): Thomas edits a newsroom article, reviews a story, cracks a case, and runs a campaign. Pushed to a Grade 5–6 stretch; skills show through *doing*, not A/B/C/D. | Thomas takes this to confirm the diagnosis with real evidence. |
| 3 | [`03-answer-key-and-rubrics.md`](03-answer-key-and-rubrics.md) | **Answer key + rubrics** and the score→domain mapping. | Use right after the test to score it. |
| 4 | [`04-test-plan.md`](04-test-plan.md) | The **test plan** — administration, timing, scoring, how raw scores become a diagnostic profile, and the re-test cadence for progress monitoring. | Use to run the test fairly and to track progress over time. |
| 5 | [`05-study-plan-agent.md`](05-study-plan-agent.md) | The **study-plan agent** — a reusable AI tutor/planner definition that takes the test results and produces adaptive weekly study plans for Thomas. | Use after scoring to generate and run the ongoing plan. |

## The 60-second summary

1. Thomas is academically strong; **reading is his relative growth area** (it lags
   his math). The goal is not remediation — it's **moving a good reader toward
   excellent**, especially in *inference, evidence, and written response*, which are
   the skills that NWEA scores tend to under-reveal.
2. The weighted diagnosis predicts his **likely** strengths/weaknesses; the test
   **confirms** them with evidence.
3. The study-plan agent then turns the confirmed profile into a **time-boxed,
   weight-driven plan** and re-checks every 4–6 weeks.

> **Honesty note:** Items 1–2 are *hypotheses* until Thomas takes the test. A single
> overall RIT score cannot pinpoint sub-skills — that is the entire reason for the
> diagnostic. Treat the weights as a starting bet, and let his real answers update them.
