## B. Rubrics for constructed responses

### 2-point short-response items (Q3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 15, 16)

| Pts | Criteria |
|----|----------|
| **2** | Answers the question correctly **and** includes a relevant detail/phrase from the text. |
| **1** | Correct idea but **no text evidence**, OR evidence given but explanation is vague/incomplete. |
| **0** | Incorrect, blank, or unrelated to the text. |

**Sample full-credit answers**
- **Q3:** Maya is becoming more confident / willing to trust herself. Evidence: she says
  "I've got this one" even though Dani "had a stronger leg."
- **Q5:** At first a hero = someone who scores ("Heroes…were the ones who scored"). By the
  end, a hero = someone who *believes their effort is enough* ("she believed they were
  enough"). The change is from outside praise → inner belief.
- **Q7:** Phrase like *"three thousand people holding their breath"* or *"as still as a
  held breath"*; shows pressure because it emphasizes silence/tension before the kick.
- **Q11:** The example of **sweat drying on your skin and feeling cooler**; good because
  it's something the reader has personally felt, making an abstract science idea concrete.
- **Q13:** **cause & effect**; detail: hot air rises → escapes through the top *while*
  cooler air is drawn in from below (one causes the other).
- **Q15:** Misting needs water, and "in dry regions" that water "is already scarce," so
  comfort competes with conserving a limited resource.
- **Q16:** Passive cooling "happens almost without any machines" / uses little electricity,
  while misting consumes water — so passive cooling saves resources.

### 8 / 33 — Constructed Response (6 points)

| Trait | 2 pts | 1 pt | 0 pts |
|-------|-------|------|-------|
| **Claim/topic sentence** | Clear statement of the problem chosen | Present but vague | Missing |
| **Text evidence** | Accurate, relevant detail from the chosen passage | Weak/partly inaccurate detail | None |
| **Explanation & structure** | Explains how evidence supports the point; organized, complete sentences | Some explanation; minor organization issues | Little/no explanation |

*(Q8 in the test booklet is the Grade-5 stretch literature item; "33" is the writing item.
Score the writing item here.)*

### 34 — Short Opinion Response (4 points)

| Trait | 2 pts | 1 pt | 0 pts |
|-------|-------|------|-------|
| **Clear opinion** | States a clear position | Position unclear | No opinion |
| **Two reasons** | Two distinct, sensible reasons | Only one reason | No reasons |

*(Conventions are checked separately in Part 4, so don't double-penalize spelling here —
note it, but score for ideas.)*

### Q8 — Grade-5 stretch (2 pts, counts in Part 1)

Full credit: recognizes the author left out the kick to **focus on the feeling/suspense**
or to put the reader *inside Maya's experience* (it felt like a blur), creating drama. 1 pt
for a partial idea, 0 if blank/off-track.

### Interpreting each domain %

| % Mastery | Reading as | Study-plan response |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **90–100%** | Strength — maintain | Light enrichment only |
| **70–89%** | Solid, polish edges | Weekly light practice |
| **50–69%** | Developing — focus area | Primary weekly target |
| **< 50%** | Priority gap | Front-load, re-teach, re-test sooner |

Multiply each domain's *need* by its **weight from file 01** to decide the order the study
plan attacks them. The agent in file 05 does this automatically when you give it the scores.
