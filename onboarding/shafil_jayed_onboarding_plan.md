## Section 5 — Notes for Tom (Buddy Guide)

1. **Be patient with logistics vocabulary.** Shafil is tech-smart but logistics terms (PO, ASN, GRN, freight carrier, bill of lading) will be new. Define them the first time you use them.
2. **Lean into his tech skills early.** If the spreadsheet is a mess, let Shafil clean it up in Week 2–3. It builds ownership and delivers real value.
3. **Don't just show — narrate the why.** Tell Shafil *why* you do each step the way you do it. He'll learn faster and flag process gaps you haven't noticed.
4. **Flag problems early.** If Shafil is falling behind, missing tasks, or making repeated errors, let the manager know by Week 2 — don't wait until the 30-day review.
5. **Celebrate small wins.** First solo shipment received correctly, first vendor email sent — acknowledge these. It builds confidence.
