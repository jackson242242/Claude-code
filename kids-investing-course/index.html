MISSION 1

🌱 Spend, Save, or Plant?

It's your birthday! 🎂 Grandma gives you $10. You have three choices. Try all three to see what happens to your money after 5 years!

MISSION 4

🧺 Don't Put All Your Eggs in One Basket

Investments don't only go UP — sometimes they go DOWN. That's called risk. Smart investors spread their money out. Let's test it! You have 100 coins.

🎉 You discovered diversification! (A fancy word for "spread it out.") With ONE basket, one bad year can smash everything. With FOUR baskets, a winner usually makes up for a loser. Less scary, more steady!
