# Aludia — IG DM / 评论快捷回复模板 v1（2026-07-11）

> 用法：IG 专业账户 → 设置 → 快捷回复（Saved Replies），逐条存入。
> 占位符 [ ] 由老板按实际情况替换。DM 响应速度 < 1 小时是转化关键。

## DM 模板
1. **问价**（关键词 price）
   > Hi love! 🤍 The [产品名] is $[价格] — you can see all details and
   > order here: [链接]. Happy to help you pick if you're deciding between
   > pieces!
2. **问运费/时效**（shipping）
   > We ship from New York within [X] business days — standard shipping is
   > [$X / free over $X], and you'll get tracking the moment it's out. 📦
3. **问材质/过敏**（material）
   > Great question! The [产品名] is [材质，如 925 silver / 14k gold
   > plated brass]. It's [hypoallergenic/nickel-free — 仅在属实时使用].
   > Care tips come with every order 🤍
4. **问尺寸**（size）
   > Our [rings/necklaces] come in [尺码范围]. Quick tip: [一句量法，如
   > wrap a string around your finger and measure it in mm]. Between two
   > sizes? Go [up/down] — 有疑问发照片我们帮你看!
5. **问退换**（return）
   > We accept [returns/exchanges] within [X] days, unworn and in original
   > packaging. Just DM us your order number and we'll sort it out — no
   > drama, promise 🤍
6. **催单/查件**（where is my order）
   > So sorry for the wait! Send me your order number and I'll check right
   > away. （查完给真实状态——绝不编造物流信息）
7. **合作邀约**（collab/influencer）
   > Thank you for thinking of us! 🤍 Please email [邮箱] with your media
   > kit and we'll take a look. （给邮箱统一收口，避免 DM 里散谈）

## 评论区快捷回复
- 夸奖类：`Thank you love! 🤍 This one's the [产品名] if you want a closer
  look ✨`（顺手报产品名——评论区是免费广告位）
- 问价类：`Just DM'd you the details! 💌`（价格引到 DM，既合规又开启私聊）
- CTA 应答（"✨"）：`Sent! Check your DMs 💌`

## 红线
- 库存/物流/材质**只说真实情况**；不确定就查完再回。
- 过敏声明（hypoallergenic）只在材质确实支持时使用——这是法务级红线。
