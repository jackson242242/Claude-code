## Verified facts (checked this run)
- 肉夹馍 = ellipsis of 「肉夹于馍」 (meat clasped in the bun); default 肉夹馍 means 腊汁肉
  夹白吉馍. — 维基百科/百度百科·肉夹馍
- 腊汁肉 dates back ~2,000 years to the Qin era; in the Warring States it was called
  「寒肉」, made in the Han state (韩) of the Qin-Jin-Yu triangle; after Qin conquered Han
  the technique passed into Chang'an. — 澎湃/唯典·肉夹馍历史
- 隋唐时称「馍夹肉」, refined; 明清 further spread to the streets. — 澎湃
- 白吉馍 craft = 「铁圈虎背菊花心」, three-ring pattern, thin crisp skin, soft interior. —
  唯典/人民日报·陕西馆
- Modern one-piece form: 樊炳仁 (Lantian, Shaanxi) came to Xi'an in 1904 selling 腊汁肉
  street-side; his son 樊凤祥 saw porters needing a one-handed meal and combined meat +
  馍 into today's rou jia mo. — 澎湃/唯典
- 馍 & 肉 developed independently for a long time before being joined. — 唯典
