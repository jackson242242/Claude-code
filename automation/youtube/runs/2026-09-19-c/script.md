## Verified figures (this run, 2026)
- **Budget/backpacker:** ~$30–70/day → a comfortable week ≈ **$300–500**. (chinaxiantour, budgetyourtrip)
- **Mid-range:** ~$90–165/day → ≈ $560–1,050/week. (windhorsetour, unusualnomad)
- Hostel dorm bed **$5–11/night**; street-food meal **$1.50–3**; hand-pulled noodles **under $2**; metro **$0.40–1**; a bullet train that costs a fortune in Europe is often **$40–90**; many historic sites free or $5–40. (multiple 2026 guides)
