## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Only ~30% of a cow's meat is suitable for Chaoshan beef hotpot. — Baidu Baike (EN), The World of Chinese
- Beef reaches the shop within ~3 hours of slaughter; slaughter-to-table can be as little as 4 hours; never frozen. — Go China Freely, China Wonders Guide
- Each cut blanched to its own exact time; some cuts 3–8s, common ~12s. — The World of Chinese, chinese-recipe.com
- Premium marbled neck cut (脖仁 / "snowflake") yields <0.2% of a whole cow. — Go China Freely
- Clear/light broth (no chili); cattle usually 2–4yr yellow cattle. — China Wonders Guide
