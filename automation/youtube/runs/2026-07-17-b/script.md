## Verified facts (all this run)
- 1904: French missionary Tian Dineng (田德能) brought coffee saplings from Vietnam, planted them
  by the Zhukula (朱苦拉) village church, Binchuan county, Dali, Yunnan — regarded as mainland
  China's earliest coffee source. (大理文旅 / 大理日报 / 咖啡金融网)
- The grove: 22 century-old trees + ~1,134 trees averaging ~70 years = China's oldest coffee grove;
  pure Bourbon/Typica small-bean. (大理日报)
- Yi (彝族) village, ~84 households / 378 people; old hand-craft — hand-pick, sun-dry, pan-roast,
  stone-grind, fire-brew — named a provincial intangible-heritage item in 2017. (大理文旅)
