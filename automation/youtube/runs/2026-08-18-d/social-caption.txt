China Travel Expert 🧭 | Stop four on the Qinghai-Gansu loop is a lake that shouldn't exist — it was an industrial mine first.

Emerald Lake near Dachaidan (Qinghai, ~3,200m) formed from the flooded pits of a boron-ore mine. Magnesium, potassium and lithium paint each pool cyan, emerald and deep blue, and on a still morning the sky and snow peaks mirror twice over. Reward at the end of the drive: a bowl of Qinghai lamb noodles. Expert tip: come windless at dawn for the cleanest mirror.
