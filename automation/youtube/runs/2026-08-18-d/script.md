## Facts (verified this run)
- Emerald Lake, near Dachaidan (Da Qaidam), Haixi, Qinghai; **~3,200m** elevation, **~15 km²**, ~700km from Xining.
- Formed from **mining pits of the Dachaidan Chemical Plant's boron-ore extraction** (a former industrial mine).
- Magnesium-sulfate salt lake; **potassium, magnesium, lithium** + halides → alternating **cyan / emerald / deep-blue** pools.
- Reflects sky, clouds and the snow-capped Qaidam peaks ("sky mirror").
