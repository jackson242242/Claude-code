## Honest framing
The two base tickets are nearly equal ($33 vs $35), but Yosemite's $35 is **per vehicle** (a whole carful) while Zhangjiajie's ¥236 is **per person** — so per person Yosemite is cheaper. The fresh 2026 twist flips it again: foreigners now pay +$100 at Yosemite. The point is honest: China is not automatically the cheaper park. (FX ~7.1 CNY/USD.)
