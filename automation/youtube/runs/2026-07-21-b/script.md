## Verified facts (checked this run)
- 热干面 originated early-1930s Hankou; vendor 李包 boiled noodles, tossed them in oil
  and air-dried (拌油晾干); folk tale: an overturned oil jug spilled onto the noodles,
  and reheated next day they smelled wonderful. — 维基百科·热干面 / 硚口城纪 / 澎湃
- Around 1937, 蔡明纬 added pepper, radish pickle and thick sticky 芝麻酱 (sesame
  paste) → a filling "饱感面." — genealogies.net / 武汉商务局
- After 1945 (WWII victory), 蔡明纬 and brother 蔡明经 opened a shop at 满春路口 under
  two old 楝 trees; name 「蔡林记」 = 明纬+明经 「双木成林」, wishing the business to grow
  deep-rooted like the trees. — genealogies.net / 新浪
- Breakfast in Wuhan dialect = 「过早」; hot-dry noodles are a top 过早 choice for being
  fast and tasty. — 百度百科·热干面 / 武汉商务局
- 拌面手法 "深翻浅覆、高挑低搅" (toss high, fold shallow) to coat every strand. — sohu 综述
