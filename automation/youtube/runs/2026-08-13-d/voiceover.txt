This street lets you walk in the rain — no umbrella. They're called qilou, arcade houses. Upper floors reach out over the sidewalk. Sun or downpour, your walk stays dry. Enning Road — Guangzhou's longest arcade, built 1931. In Cantonese, rain is lok jyu. Xiguan locals still live above these shops. Stop three on our Guangzhou heritage route. Expert Tip: come in a light drizzle, dry arcade, glossy stone, no crowds. Next stop: morning dim sum on Shangxiajiu — the route's reward.
