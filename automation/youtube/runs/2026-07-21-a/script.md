## Verified facts (checked this run)
- 上海早餐「四大金刚」= 大饼 (flatbread, sweet or savoury) · 油条 (hollow crispy fried
  dough) · 粢饭 (glutinous rice, often wrapping a youtiao, ball or cake form) · 豆浆
  (soy milk, sweet or savoury). — 百度百科·四大金刚 / 虹口区政府 / 机核 GCORES
- The four items trace to late-Qing / early-Republic era as migrants brought regional
  food cultures to Shanghai after the port opened. — 文小白 / sohu 综述
- A Nov 13, 1936 《社会日报》 article already describes 6–8 AM queues for 大饼/油条/豆浆.
  — 机核 GCORES (引 1936 报刊)
- The label 「四大金刚」 (originally the Four Heavenly Kings of Buddhism, protective
  deities) was applied to this breakfast set and popularised in the 1980s. — 百度百科
