## Verified facts (sourced this run 2026-08-11)
- "未有羊城，先有光孝" — the Guangxiao Temple site predates Guangzhou city; the temple has ~1,700 years of history (site was a Western Han residence of a Nanyue-kingdom prince before becoming a temple).
- The Sixth Patriarch of Chan/Zen Buddhism, Huineng (慧能), was tonsured (ordained) here; the Hair-Burial Pagoda (瘗发塔, Tang dynasty) marks where his hair was buried.
