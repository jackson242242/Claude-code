They say Chongqing has no flat ground — the whole city is built between mountains and the Yangtze, and even GPS gets lost here. A building's ground floor can be someone else's tenth. A monorail bursts straight through an apartment block; Liziba station was built like this in 2004, on China's first straddle monorail, and the train clears the building in eight seconds. To cross the river you ride a cable car — the Yangtze's first sky corridor, the locals' sky bus. Climbing slopes is daily life; deep in an alley a pot of red hotpot boils, and one word — yao-de — seals the deal. At dusk, Hongyadong lights up like the town in Spirited Away. Mountains never tire of height, nor rivers of depth. Chongqing turned "hard to walk" into a view. Not a city — a maze in three dimensions.
