## Facts + sources (verified this run 2026-07-10)
- 英文 China = 国家；小写 china = 瓷器；名源与景德镇制瓷史相连。
  - MudandLeaves《Jingdezhen Porcelain and the name China》 https://mudandleaves.com/blogs/teatime/jingdezhen-porcelain-and-the-name-china
- 景德镇为「千年瓷都」，制瓷逾千年；一件器物历经「七十二道工序」，可经数十/七十二双手。
  - Farmerleaf《Jingdezhen Porcelain》 https://farmerleaf.com/pages/jingdezhen-porcelain
  - Oriental Artisan《The White Gold of the East》 https://oriental-artisan.com/blogs/knowledge/the-white-gold-of-the-east-a-collector-s-guide-to-authentic-jingdezhen-porcelain
- 高岭土（Kaolin）名源于景德镇城外高岭村；富含氧化铝，撑起瓷体结构。釉后二次入窑约 1300°C 使胎釉玻化。
  - saanwu《The Truth About Kaolin》 https://saanwu.com/blogs/news-on-porcelain-tea-set-ceramics-ware-and-sharing-tea-culture/the-truth-about-kaolin-the-secret-behind-jingdezhen-porcelain
- 「景德」为北宋真宗年号（公元1004年起），赐名景德镇。
  - 景德镇 — 中国文联《Jingdezhen: Millennium Porcelain Capital》 https://www.cflac.org.cn/ArtExchange/yishujiaoliu201802/yishujiaoliuzazhi201802/201901/t20190109_432013.htm
