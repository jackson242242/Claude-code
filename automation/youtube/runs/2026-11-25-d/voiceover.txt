Owe a fortune, and you have two choices: disappear, or pay it back in public. Luo Yonghao chose public.

He ran a Chinese phone company, Smartisan. When it ran out of money in 2018, he said he personally owed more than 600 million yuan — about 90 million US dollars.

Most would file for bankruptcy. Instead, in 2020, he started selling things on a livestream. His first show sold over 100 million yuan in three hours.

By 2024, he said he'd repaid more than the original debt — though new lawsuits kept the total climbing. But he kept showing up, night after night.

So — would you livestream your way out of that, or disappear?
