## Facts & sources (verified this run, 2026-07-11)
- Pingjiang Road (平江路) is a well-preserved historic district in Gusu, Suzhou; the main
  street runs **1,606 m** along Pingjiang He and appears on the **1229 map 《平江图》
  (Pingjiang Tu)**. Added to China's **National Historic and Cultural Streets** list in
  **2015**. Teahouses host **Suzhou Pingtan** (评弹) ballad-storytelling; nearby are the
  Kunqu Opera Museum and the Suzhou Pingtan Museum. Local saying: "一条平江路，半座姑苏城."
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingjiang_Road
  - https://www.travelchinaguide.com/attraction/jiangsu/suzhou/pingjiang-road.htm
- Tang couplet "君到姑苏见，人家尽枕河" — 杜荀鹤《送人游吴》 (classic, describes Suzhou's
  water-town houses "pillowed on the river").
- "上有天堂，下有苏杭" — classic proverb pairing Suzhou & Hangzhou with paradise.
