He climbed all Five Great Mountains of China — then said the search was over. The one that made him stop was Huangshan. Eighty-eight peaks over a thousand meters, clouds rolling into a sea below your feet, pines growing straight out of bare rock. One greeting pine has waited a thousand years; at dawn, the sea of clouds turns gold. Xu Xiake said it best — after Huangshan, no peak remains.
