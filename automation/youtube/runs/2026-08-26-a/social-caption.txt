China Travel Expert 🧭 | In Chinese calligraphy there's no undo — the ink hits the paper once.

The whole art runs on the Four Treasures: brush, ink, paper, inkstone. The brush never lands the same way twice — which is why, in 353 CE, a slightly drunk Wang Xizhi dashed off the Orchid Pavilion Preface and, legend says, could never match it again once sober. Still called China's finest running script.

Expert Tip: if you try it in China, ask for xuan paper (宣纸) — it drinks ink like nothing else.
