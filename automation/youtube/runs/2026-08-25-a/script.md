## Verified facts + sources
- English "china" = porcelain; Jingdezhen blue-and-white came to symbolize China abroad
  (Ming, Zheng He voyages 1405–1433 carried it as gifts). — chinasage.info/porcelain.htm ·
  mudandleaves.com (Jingdezhen porcelain and the name China)
- True porcelain is fired ~1250–1350°C for full vitrification/translucency; kaolin is
  thermostable to ~1300°C. — sinocultural.com (Chinese ceramic firing) · cheralle.com (kaolin)
- Kaolin = "china clay", named after the village Gāolǐng (Gaoling) near Jingdezhen; new kaolin
  clay found in the Yuan era revolutionized production. — cheralle.com · chinasage.info
- Jingdezhen: center of imperial porcelain for 1,000+ years (since the Song dynasty). — chinasage.info
