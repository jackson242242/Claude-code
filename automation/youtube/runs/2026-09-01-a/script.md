## Verified facts (sources this run)
- Chinese name 景泰蓝 Jingtailan = "Jingtai Blue"; 蓝 (blue) the most common ground color; named for the Ming Jingtai reign (1449–1457). — visitbeijing.com.cn; baike.baidu.com/en (Cloisonne)
- Technique introduced from Persia in the Yuan (13th c.), matured under Ming Jingtai; expanded in Qing (Kangxi/Qianlong). — visitbeijing; artsofasia; newhanfu
- Process: copper roughcast → flat copper wires soldered into patterns (掐丝) → enamel paste filled between wires → kiln-fired, repeated & polished. — chinesenotes; baike.baidu.com/en
- One of China's national intangible cultural heritage metal crafts; 600+ year history. — baike.baidu.com/en; newhanfu
