China Travel Expert 🧭 | China's best morning barely costs a thing — and tourists walk right past it

Hot take: skip the pricey scenic-area ticket for one morning and walk into a working temple at dawn. Thousands of Chinese temples charge a yuan or nothing and hand you free incense at the gate — a Guangzhou temple gives three free joss sticks, and Hangzhou's Lingyin has been free to enter since Dec 2025. Go 7–9am and you'll see locals and monks actually worshipping, not a tour queue.

Tip: take incense with your right hand, bow, never point the lit end at people.
