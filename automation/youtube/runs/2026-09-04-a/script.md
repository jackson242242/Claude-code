## Verified facts (WebSearch this run)
- Many active Chinese temples are free or nearly free and hand out free joss sticks:
  a Guangzhou temple charges ¥1 and gives three free sticks; Shanghai's Chenghuang
  (City God) Temple and Beijing's Lama Temple give incense free at entry; Hangzhou's
  Lingyin Temple has been FREE to enter since December 2025.
- Active temples keep monks and locals worshipping alongside visitors (authentic).
- Early morning (6-9am) is the most auspicious time and when locals actually come.
- Contrast: major ticketed scenic/temple sights command premium prices (¥100s), unlike
  the ¥1 / free neighbourhood temples.
