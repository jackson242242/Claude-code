## Verified facts (sourced this run 2026-08-11)
- Mogao Caves: 735 caves total; 492 contain murals and painted sculptures; murals cover ~45,000 m²; ~2,415 painted clay sculptures.
- First cave excavated 366 CE (monk Yue Zun); construction/painting spanned ~10–11 dynasties (Northern Liang through Yuan), roughly a thousand years.
- The murals were made by generations of anonymous artisans (画工) whose individual names were not recorded — a broad, well-documented feature of Chinese craft tradition.
- Practical: Mogao caps daily visitors and requires advance online reservation; special ("特窟") caves need extra booking. (Framed as general, verifiable visitor guidance.)
