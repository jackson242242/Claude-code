## Facts (verified this run)
- Fortified towers (diaolou) blending Chinese + Western architecture, built by overseas Chinese (Wuyi/Kaiping).
- Peak **over 3,000**; **~1,833 remain**; 2007 UNESCO inscription covers **five village clusters / ~1,800 towers**.
- Emigrants went to the Americas, Southeast Asia, Africa; returned with wealth + new architectural styles.
- Fusion: Baroque balustrades, Romanesque arches, medieval crenellations, Islamic-inspired domes beside upswept Chinese eaves.
- Built for defence against flooding and banditry.
