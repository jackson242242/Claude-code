China Travel Expert 🧭 | They crossed the Pacific, made their fortunes, and came home to build fortresses like nothing else in China.

The Kaiping Diaolou in Guangdong were raised by overseas Chinese who returned rich with foreign ideas. Roman domes, Greek columns and Baroque railings sit above grey village brick — over 3,000 were built, about 1,800 remain, UNESCO-listed in 2007. Locals called the returnees "Gold Mountain uncles." Expert tip: go to Zili village for the best-preserved cluster.
