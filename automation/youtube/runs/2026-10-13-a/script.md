## Claim (single): the Leshan Giant Buddha survived ~1,200 years not by luck but by a hidden drainage system built into it.

## Verified facts (this run, 2+ sources)
- 71 m tall; the largest / tallest stone Buddha in the world. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leshan_Giant_Buddha; ancient-history-sites.com]
- Tang dynasty; carving began ~713 CE (monk Hai Tong), completed 803 CE (~90 years). [ancient-history-sites.com; album-online caption]
- 1,021 spiralled hair curls; hidden drainage pipes/gutters carved into the head (behind the ears), and into the robe/clothes, still working — carry off rainwater to reduce weathering. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leshan_Giant_Buddha; govt.chinadaily.com.cn Giant Buddha Leshan]
- Sits at the confluence of the Min, Dadu and Qingyi rivers (Lingyun Hill). [wikipedia]
