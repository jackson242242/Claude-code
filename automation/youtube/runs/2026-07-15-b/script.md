## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Over 80% of cafés in China operate independently. (Global Times / Barista Magazine)
- Third-wave coffee in China emphasises origin, processing, and manual brewing;
  specialty single-origin advancing ~6.97% CAGR. (Mordor Intelligence; Barista Magazine)
- First crack (一爆) is the most important roasting milestone — after it, every
  second defines flavor, body, and character; roasters read sound + time + temp +
  color + smell together. (Coffee Crafters — First & Second Crack guide)
- Independent Shanghai/China cafés commonly roast in-house and champion Yunnan
  single origin (e.g. Seesaw's own Yunnan roastery). (Perfect Daily Grind)
