## Verified facts (two sources each, this run 2026-10-07)
- Since **2017**, nearly every museum / scenic area / theme park in China uses **real-name ticketing**; China's top 5A sites and the Forbidden City require **advance online reservation, typically 1-7 days ahead**, and on-site walk-up sales are largely suspended. Tickets usually release **7 days before**; latest booking is the day before.
  - Hidden China Travel: https://hiddenchinatravel.com/why-china-attractions-require-reservations
  - China Services Info (gov): https://www.chinaservicesinfo.com/s/202502/20/WS67b6d4de498eec7e1f72fce7/tickets-and-activities-booking-in-china.html
- **Official apps / mini-programs often require a Chinese ID or phone number** and reject foreign-passport online payment (Forbidden City, Summer Palace, etc.). Workaround: book on **Trip.com / Meituan international / Alipay "Scenic Area Ticket Booking"**, or via an international travel agency, using your passport.
  - China Services Info (gov): (same as above)
  - China Xian Tour — tips for expats: https://www.chinaxiantour.com/travel-guide/tips-for-expats-travel
- Consequence if unbooked: **turned away at the entrance** — there is no reliable same-day window at the busiest sites.
  - Hidden China Travel (above) + Into Travel China: https://intotravelchina.com/answer/questioncontent/74.html
