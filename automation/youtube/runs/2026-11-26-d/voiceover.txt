At her peak, she simply stopped. Li Ziqi made quiet videos of farm life, cooking, and old crafts, one of the most-followed Chinese-language creators in the world.

In early 2021 she set a Guinness World Record for a Chinese-language YouTube channel: over fourteen million subscribers, later more than twenty million.

Then, in July 2021, the videos stopped, amid a reported dispute with her agency over her own name. For three years, silence.

In November 2024, she returned. One new video passed a hundred million views within hours.

So, walk away at the very top, or keep posting? Which would you have done?
