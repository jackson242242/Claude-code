## Verified facts (WebSearch 2026-07-26; sources in meta description)
- 徐州"伏羊食俗" listed as **国家级非遗 (national intangible cultural heritage) in 2021**.
- "以热制热": eating hot mutton + spicy oil/vinegar/coriander during the 三伏 (dog days) makes the body
  sweat profusely to expel internal heat — a 冬病夏治 idea akin to 三伏贴. (新华网江苏 2024-07-31)
- **彭祖** (founder of 徐州/彭城) is revered as **中华烹饪始祖 (ancestor of Chinese cooking)**; created
  羊方藏鱼 ("天下第一菜"). (百度百科 伏羊节 / 中国华文教育网)
- Since 2004 Xuzhou has held the **彭祖伏羊节** annually; 2024 was the **21st** edition (opened 2024-07-14),
  reviving the "万人空巷吃伏羊" custom and "点亮夏日经济". (腾讯新闻 2024-07-14 / 新华网江苏 2024-07-31)

  not health advice); no endorsement; zero politics.
