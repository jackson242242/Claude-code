## Facts + sources (verified this run)
- Li River scenery between Guilin and Yangshuo (~83 km) is the artwork on the ¥20 note; the exact scene is near Xingping — "Reflection of Yellow Cloth Shoal" near Nine-Horse Fresco Hill; most-photographed spot; best via bamboo raft.
- Cormorant fishing: trained birds with a throat band; long Li River tradition, now fading and largely a tourist show (framed honestly as "fading").
- Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉): history traced ~2,000 years to Qin-dynasty soldiers; rice-flour noodles + slow-boiled broth (cinnamon, ginger, tangerine peel, bones) + self-serve toppings.
