## Facts (all sourced this run 2026-09-05)
- China HSR = world's largest network, over 45,000 km of track (end of 2023).
  Top speed 350 km/h. Beijing–Shanghai (~1,300 km) in ~4.5–6 hours. [chinaguidelines]
- Booking 2026: the official **12306** app now lets foreigners register with a
  passport (email works; no Chinese phone number needed) — select "外国护照"
  (Foreign Passport) and upload the passport photo. Pay with Visa / Mastercard /
  JCB / Diners, or a linked Alipay/WeChat. At the station, tap your passport at the
  orange gate — no paper ticket. [realchinatrip 2026 / chinafortravelers 2026]
- Tickets release 14 days before the travel date (including travel day). [trip.com]
