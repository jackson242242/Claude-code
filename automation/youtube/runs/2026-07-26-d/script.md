## Verified facts (WebSearch 2026-07-26; sources in meta description)
- In Chaoshan, congee is called **"糜" (muê)**, and the clay-pot version is the **star of the 夜宵 (late-night)
  scene** — 蟹粥/虾蟹海鲜粥/生鱼粥/甲鱼粥/蚝粥 etc. (百度百科 潮汕砂锅粥 · 果壳 guokr 460862)
- Method (生滚): the pot simmers raw rice; when the congee hits the right point, **fresh seafood is added whole**
  so its sweetness melts into the rice while the seafood stays tender. (下厨房 · 知乎 p/436272105)
- Texture is distinct from Cantonese (Guangfu) congee: Chaoshan 糜 shows **distinct rice grains** — "似烂非烂"
  (half-broken), not smoothed to a paste. (知乎 p/436272105)
- 地都青蟹粥 (Didu green-crab congee) is one of the most famous. (果壳 460862)
