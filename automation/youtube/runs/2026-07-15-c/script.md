## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Chengdu had ~7,900 coffee shops by 2022 — 2nd nationally after Shanghai;
  ~447 new shops opened in H1-2022 (~2.4/day). (果壳; 川观智库 成都咖啡馆观察报告)
- Chengdu has 10,000+ teahouses; 盖碗茶 uses the 三才 set — lid = heaven (盖为天),
  saucer = earth (托为地), bowl = people (碗为人). (百度百科 成都茶馆; hk01)
- Coffee + gaiwan tea coexist in the same slow-paced 巴适 street culture. (36氪; hk01)
