## Verified facts (2 sources; legend honestly flagged)
- Origin legend ~**1,800 years** (Eastern Han): physician **Zhang Zhongjing** wrapped lamb + warming herbs in dough shaped like ears — **娇耳 "tender ears"** — to treat villagers' **frostbitten ears**. **Framed as legend** ("Legend says"); the anecdote appears only in later texts, no independent proof he invented dumplings. — chinaeducationaltours.com; Museum of Food and Culture; Wikipedia *Jiaozi*.
- **交子 homophone:** jiaozi (饺子) echoes **交子** (the turn between the old and new year, 子时) → eaten at **New Year's Eve midnight**.
- **元宝 shape:** folded to resemble old **silver ingots**, a wish for wealth/luck.
