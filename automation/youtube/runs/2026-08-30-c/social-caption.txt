China Travel Expert 🧭 | By legend, Chinese dumplings weren't food first — they were medicine. ~1,800 years ago a doctor wrapped lamb and warming herbs in ear-shaped dough to cure frostbitten ears. The name jiaozi echoes 交子, the turn of the year — so families fold them at New Year's midnight, shaped like silver ingots for luck. Expert tip: pleat the edge so they don't burst in the boil.
