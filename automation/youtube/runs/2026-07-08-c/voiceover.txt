Twelve point nine million. That's how many students just sat China's gaokao — the single most high-stakes exam on the planet.

Over two grueling days this June, one test largely decides which university, if any, a student gets into. No essays about your summer internship. No holistic review of your hobbies. For the vast majority of applicants, it comes down to one number: your score. Years of studying, compressed into two days.

And here's the twist most headlines miss — this year's 12.9 million is actually down from 13.35 million the year before, as China's graduate job market shifts and some families start rethinking the traditional path.

But make no mistake: for the millions who sat it, the gaokao is still the biggest two days of their lives. Which country's exam system would you survive? Follow for more on how the world tests its students.
