# Slot c (Short, 9:16) — 12.9 Million Teenagers Just Took the Hardest Exam on Earth

**Pillar:** world-colleges · **publishAt:** 2026-07-08T22:00:00Z

## Hook
12.9 million teenagers. One exam. Two days. No retakes.

## Spoken script (~150 words)
Twelve point nine million. That's how many students just sat China's gaokao — the
single most high-stakes exam on the planet. Over two grueling days this June, one
test largely decides which university, if any, a student gets into. No essays about
your summer internship. No holistic review of your hobbies. For the vast majority
of applicants, it comes down to one number: your score. Years of studying,
compressed into two days. And here's the twist most headlines miss — this year's
12.9 million is actually down from 13.35 million the year before, as China's
graduate job market shifts and some families start rethinking the traditional path.
But make no mistake: for the millions who sat it, the gaokao is still the biggest
two days of their lives. Which country's exam system would you survive? Follow for
more on how the world tests its students.

## Facts & sources (verified this run — 2026-07-08)
- 12.9 million candidates registered for China's 2026 gaokao (Ministry of Education).
- Down from 13.35 million registrants the previous year.
- Held June 7-8, 2026 (some regions extended through June 10).
- The gaokao is the primary gateway to higher education in China.
