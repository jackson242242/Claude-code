Number three: staying inside the lines. Transit is not a nationwide pass — each entry port comes with a permitted region, and you are expected to stay within it for your whole visit. Enter through the Shanghai area, and you're generally free to roam the Yangtze River Delta — Shanghai, and the neighbouring provinces around it. Enter somewhere else, and your allowed zone is drawn differently. Plan a spontaneous side trip that crosses out of your zone, and you've quietly broken the terms of your own entry. And it goes further: not every airport in China is even eligible for transit. Some, like Lhasa in Tibet and Urumchi in the far west, are simply not on the transit list at all — land there expecting visa-free, and you'll have a problem. The fix is pure homework: before you book a single hotel, confirm that your entry port and every city on your itinerary sit inside the same permitted region. Ten minutes with the official list saves your whole trip.
