Number two: the clock myth — and for once, the rule is actually in your favour. Your two-hundred-and-forty hours do not start the moment your wheels touch the runway. The count officially begins at midnight on the day after you clear immigration. So if you land on a Monday afternoon, that entire Monday doesn't burn against your ten days — the clock only starts ticking at midnight. That's most of a bonus day that a lot of travelers never realize they've been handed. Use it. What the clock does not buy you, though, is any change to what you're allowed to do: visa-free means tourism and short business, and that's the whole list. No paid work, no enrolling in a course, no journalism on assignment. Treat the ten days as generous — because they are — but count them from the correct midnight, and whatever you do, never schedule your outbound flight for the very last hour of the very last day. Give yourself a buffer.
