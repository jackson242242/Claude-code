And number one — the single biggest mistake of all, the one that quietly undoes everything else: assuming any of this is frozen in place. It is not. Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam were added to the transit list on August twentieth, twenty twenty-six. The United Kingdom and Canada joined the thirty-day list back in February. New entry ports keep opening — Guangzhou, the city of Zhuhai, the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge. Countries get added; occasionally conditions get tightened. This is exactly why I told you at the start to verify. The single smartest thing you can do before any China trip is open the official National Immigration Administration website, or call your embassy, within days of flying — not months before, days before — because the version of the rules that was true last month may not be the version that's true when you actually land. Anyone online who tells you the list is permanent and fixed is simply working from old information. Your job is to check the date on every rule you rely on. Do that, and you'll never be the traveler turned around at the desk.
