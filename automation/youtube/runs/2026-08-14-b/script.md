## Verified facts + sources (found this run)
- **Founded 686 AD, Tang dynasty.** Legend: Elder Huang (629–712) donated his mulberry orchard to monk Kuanghu after the trees bloomed white lotus. — Wikipedia (Kaiyuan Temple, Quanzhou); China Discovery.
- **1,300-year-old mulberry** on the grounds, the "World Mulberry King." — China Discovery / Trip.com moment.
- **Twin stone pagodas** Zhenguo (west) & Renshou (east), rebuilt in stone in the Song dynasty; tallest surviving pair of stone pagodas in China. — Top China Travel; ZONVE.
- **Survived a ~magnitude 8 earthquake in 1604**, still standing as the city's symbol. — Wikipedia.
- Part of **Quanzhou UNESCO World Heritage** ("Emporium of the World," maritime Silk Road).
- Minnan greeting **食饱未** verified in dialect bank (chinanews / wikivoyage 闽南语会话).
