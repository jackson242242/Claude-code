## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Tea-Horse Road (Chamagudao): caravan network through Yunnan/Guizhou/Sichuan mountains linking
  tea regions to Tibet, extending to Nepal/India/Myanmar. In use from the Tang dynasty (7th-10th c.)
  until the latter 20th century → over 1,000 years. [tibettravel.org; connollycove; Options/The Edge]
- Driven by Tibetan demand for tea: tea doesn't grow in the highlands and was one of the "four
  pillars of life" (with tsampa/barley, meat, butter) — it had to be imported. [tibettravel.org]
- Caravans of horses/mules carried compressed tea bricks (plus salt, herbs, silk) over passes
  soaring above 4,500 m. [tibettravel.org]
- Shaxi (Yunnan) is the only complete surviving Tea-Horse Road trading town. [Options/The Edge Singapore]
