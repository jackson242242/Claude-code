## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Luoyang, Henan. Started 493 AD (Northern Wei moved capital to Luoyang); carving continued ~400 years across seven dynasties (E/W Wei, N Qi, N Zhou, Sui, Tang, N Song).
- 100,000+ Buddha statues, 2,800 inscriptions, 2,345 niches.
- Fengxian Temple Vairocana Buddha: 17.14 m tall, head 4 m, ears ~1.9 m — largest at Longmen (Tang). Tradition: face modeled on Empress Wu Zetian (who helped fund it) — framed as legend ("They say").
- UNESCO World Heritage 2000.
