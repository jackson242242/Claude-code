## Facts (all sourced this run 2026-09-05)
- Origin: Han Dynasty (206 BC–220 AD); began as a rite to honour ancestors and pray
  for rain, later became festive entertainment. [chinahighlights]
- Dancers: usually 9; large ceremonial dragons can take up to ~50 people on the poles.
  [chinahighlights / chinaeducationaltours]
- The body is carried on wooden poles — dancers hold the poles, not the silk body.
- Construction: lightweight bamboo frame + silk; length ~25–35 m (acrobatic) up to
  ~50–70 m (parade/ceremonial). [chinaeducationaltours / danceus]
- The leader carries a rod topped by a ball — the "Pearl of Wisdom"; "Dragon Chasing
  the Pearl" symbolises the endless pursuit of wisdom. [chinahighlights]
- The longer the dragon, the more luck it is believed to bring the community.
  [chinaeducationaltours]
