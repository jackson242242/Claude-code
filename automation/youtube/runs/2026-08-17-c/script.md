## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- UNESCO World Heritage 1997; 800+ years; ~2,400m elevation, Yunnan. [UNESCO WHC 811; ChinaDiscovery]
- No city wall — deliberate; Mu-family Naxi chieftains' welcoming stance / confidence (Tea-Horse Road trade). Folk tale: surname 木 + frame = 困 (marked as tale, not fact). [OlaChina]
- Mu family ruled 1253–1723 (470 years). [OlaChina]
- Ancient water system, "one well three pools" (drink / wash food / wash clothes); snowmelt in stone channels; 300+ bridges over the Yuhe river system. [OlaChina; ChinaDiscovery]
- Naxi people; Dongba, one of the last living pictographic scripts. [UNESCO WHC 811; ChinaDiscovery]
