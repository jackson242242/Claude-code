## Verified facts + sources (this run)
- Guangzhou began demolishing city walls in 1918 and built ~40km of arcade (qilou) streets within ~10 years.
- ~36 arcade streets survive in the urban core, >20km total; Enning Road has the most intact stretch (parts 1931), Liwan District.
- Design = Paris-style arcades + Lingnan climate (rain-shelter) wisdom; Shangxiajiu a commercial hub since the 6th century.
