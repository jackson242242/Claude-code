## Single claim
The Great Wall is officially 21,196 km long — and you cannot see it from space with the naked eye.

## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Official length 21,196.18 km (13,171 mi), 2012 NCHA six-year survey — ~half the equator.
- Built/rebuilt over 2,000+ years across many dynasties.
- Not visible from space to the naked eye; Apollo→ISS astronauts confirm (low contrast, too narrow). Myth predates spaceflight (Stukeley 1754 / Ripley's 1932).
