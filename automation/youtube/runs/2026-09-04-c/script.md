## Verified facts (WebSearch this run)
- The Bund reached its golden age in the 1920s-30s — a row of banks, trading houses and
  consulates in Gothic/Baroque/Neoclassical/Renaissance/Art Deco styles, nicknamed "the
  museum of buildings"; it was the financial heart of the Far East / Asia. Decline after 1949.
- Lujiazui (Pudong), across the Huangpu, was a low-built area of residential houses,
  warehouses and factories until it became a special investment zone in 1992. Oriental
  Pearl Tower completed 1994; then Jin Mao (420.5 m), Shanghai World Financial Center
  (494 m) and Shanghai Tower (632 m) — "the world's first trio of adjacent supertall
  skyscrapers." So the tallest tower rose within ~30 years of the zone opening.
