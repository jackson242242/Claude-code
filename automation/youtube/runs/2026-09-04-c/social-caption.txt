China Travel Expert 🧭 | One river in Shanghai splits two centuries — which bank first?

West bank: the Bund, 1920s banks in European stone, once the financial heart of Asia. Straight across the Huangpu, Lujiazui was farmland and warehouses in 1990 — today it stacks the world's first trio of adjacent supertall towers, topped by the 632m Shanghai Tower. A whole future built in ~30 years, facing a century-old one across the water.

Tip: skip the cruise — the local Huangpu ferry crosses for pocket change, same skyline. Come at blue hour.
