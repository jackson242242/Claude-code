China Travel Expert 🧭 | Those dragon heads on the Forbidden City's terraces aren't decoration — they're the drains.

Rain hits the three-tier marble terrace, runs to the edge, and pours out through more than a thousand carved dragon mouths — flinging it clear so the base never floods. Pure gravity, ~600 years old, still works. Decoration or drainage — did you know?
