## Verified facts + sources (found THIS run, 2026-09-23)
- Foreign-passport holders can book directly on the official 12306 English app/site; no Chinese
  phone number or bank account required. Pay with Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners (or linked
  Alipay/WeChat). E-ticket only — tap passport at the gate; no paper ticket needed.
- Booking window: tickets open 15 days before departure, up to ~30 min before the train.
- Identity check: most passports verify automatically in minutes; foreign names often go to manual
  review (passport photo + selfie), which China State Railway says normally takes 3–5 working days.
  → hence "book early".
