China Travel Expert 🧭 | In Guangzhou, breakfast can last three hours.

Cantonese call it yum cha — "drink tea." The rule is 一盅两件: one pot of tea, two small plates of har gow, char siu bao and egg tarts. When someone pours your tea, tap two fingers on the table — a silent thank-you, a "finger kowtow" from a Qianlong-era legend. The ritual grew out of Qing-dynasty Guangzhou and became an intangible cultural heritage in 2007.

Expert Tip: go before 9am, order har gow with a pot of pu'er, and tap to say thanks.
