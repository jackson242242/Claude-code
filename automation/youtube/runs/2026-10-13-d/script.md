## Claim (single/CHOICE): China's G331 runs ~9,200 km along the northern frontier from the frozen NE to the desert NW — which end would you start?

## Verified facts (this run, 2+ sources)
- G331 runs from Dandong (Liaoning) to Altay (Xinjiang); length ~9,200 km (Wikipedia) / 9,333 km (Xinhua), the second-longest national highway in China. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_331; Xinhua via news.cn]
- Route passes the forests/snow of the northeast and the grasslands of Inner Mongolia to the deserts and Altay peaks of the far west. [CGTN; en.wikipedia]
- Part of a planned ~26,000–27,000 km "Golden Outer Ring" border loop (with G219 & G228) targeted ~2030. [yicaiglobal.com; news.cgtn.com]
