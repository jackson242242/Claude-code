## Angle
The "dirty" is the most photogenic drink in China's specialty scene: hot espresso poured
over ice-cold milk, no stirring, the dark shot bleeding through white — that mess is the
name. Curiosity-gap Short that opens on the temperature contrast, explains the look, names
the Tokyo origin, and closes on the first-sip/last-sip flavour arc + a viewer question.

## Verified facts (all this run)
- A "dirty" = hot/strong espresso poured over very cold milk; sits on top and permeates
  down, creating a messy look → the name. (Barista Magazine; Food Republic)
- Origin: Tokyo's Bear Pond Espresso, founded by Katsuyuki Tanaka (returned to Tokyo 2009);
  drink originally called "The Dirty." (Barista Magazine — interview with Tanaka)
- Flavour arc: first sip espresso-forward, last sip milk-forward. (multiple brew guides)
