China Travel Expert 🧭 | Long before ATMs, one walled town in Shanxi invented a way to move money across China without carrying a single coin.

Pingyao is one of China's best-preserved Ming-Qing towns. In 1823 it gave birth to Rishengchang — China's first draft bank: deposit silver in one city, cash a paper draft in another. 57 branches, 35 cities. The town is still ringed by a 6km Ming wall, UNESCO-listed since 1997. Expert tip: walk the wall at dawn before the crowds.
