## Facts (verified this run)
- Pingyao = outstanding Ming/Qing county town; **UNESCO 1997**.
- **Rishengchang**, established **1823**, China's **first draft bank (piaohao)** — deposit in one city, cash a paper draft in another.
- At peak: **57 branches across 35 cities**; business reached Europe & SE Asia ("Bank that Connects the World").
- City wall **>6 km**, completely encloses the town, among best-preserved in China; visible cannonball scars.
