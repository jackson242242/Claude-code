## Verified facts + sources (checked THIS run)
- Origin: **boatmen / dock workers** at Chongqing wharves (Jialing R. / Chaotianmen),
  **late Ming–early Qing**; cheap **offal + beef tallow + chili + Sichuan pepper** boiled
  in an iron pot. — CQNews; iChongqing; China Dragon Travel
- **九宫格 (nine-grid):** iron dividers so strangers sharing one pot each got a square. — CQNews
- **麻辣 (málà):** Sichuan **peppercorn numbs**, **chili burns**; basin-climate
  "dampness-expulsion" rationale. — iChongqing; TravelChina.tips
- **鸳鸯 twin pot** (Expert Tip) is a real mild/spicy split option. — TravelChina.tips
