## Verified facts (WebSearch 2026-07-26; sources in meta description)
- 红酸汤 is made from local tomatoes (毛辣果) + chili, **naturally fermented ~5-6 months, then milled and
  fermented ~another half month** → bright-red, mellow-sour, slightly spicy. **No vinegar** — the sour is
  fermentation. (光明网 2025-03-23 / 新华网 2025-05-22)
- **凯里酸汤鱼制作技艺** was listed on the **national intangible cultural heritage** list (2021). (中国非遗网 ihchina project 23598)
- 木姜子 (litsea cubeba / mountain pepper) is a signature aromatic seasoning.
- Miao/Dong folk saying "三天不吃酸，走路打蹿蹿" (three days without sour and your legs wobble) — reflects
  the 苗侗 "以酸代盐" tradition (salt was historically scarce; fermentation supplied the seasoning).
- Industrial "出圈": by 2024 黔东南's sour-soup industry **exceeded 2.5 billion yuan** total output, heading
  toward a 10-billion cluster; 玉梦(苗苗)集团 runs the country's largest fermentation base — 23 lines,
  **85,000 tons/yr** capacity. Neutral industry facts, not investment advice. (人民网/新华网 2025-05 · 新浪财经 2025-09-24)
