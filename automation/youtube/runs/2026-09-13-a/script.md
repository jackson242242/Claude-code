## Verified facts (this run)
- A single silkworm cocoon is one continuous filament ~600–900 m long (up to ~1,500 m).
  [vedantu; manitosilk]
- ~2,000 cocoons go into one silk dress (~1,000 for a shirt; 2,000–3,000 per lb raw silk).
  [selvane/woolery; manitosilk]
- China guarded sericulture for millennia; an imperial decree made smuggling silkworms or
  eggs out of the empire punishable by death; Silk-Road guards executed offenders.
  [silkroadfoundation.org; historicmysteries.com]
- The monopoly broke c. AD 550 when two Nestorian monks smuggled silkworm eggs to Byzantine
  Emperor Justinian hidden in hollow bamboo staves (and c. AD 440 a Khotan princess hid eggs
  in her headdress). [silkroadfoundation.org; projectbly.com]
