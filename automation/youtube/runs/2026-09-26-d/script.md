## Verified facts (sources in description)
- Tarim Desert Highway crosses the Taklamakan Desert, Xinjiang. Total ~552 km; ~446 km run through
  uninhabited shifting-sand dunes — the longest such desert-crossing highway in the world. Built 1995
  to serve an oil pipeline. [Wikipedia / dangerousroads]
- To stop dunes burying the road, a planted shelterbelt (rose willow, saxaul, buckthorn) runs ~436 km
  along it, 72–78 m wide, thousands of hectares, irrigated by water pumped from underground reservoirs;
  worker stations sit roughly every 4 km to tend it. [Amusing Planet / Xinhua]
