## Verified facts + sources (this run, 2026-09-21)
- Beijing – Xi'an: **1,216 km, ~4–6 hours** by high-speed rail.
  https://www.chinahighlights.com/xian/how-to-travel-from-beijing-to-xian.htm
- Xi'an – Shanghai: **~1,380 km, 6–9 hours**; trains run at up to 300 km/h.
  https://www.chinadiscovery.com/beijing-xian-shanghai-tour/transportation.html
- Beijing / Xi'an / Shanghai are the recognized "Golden Triangle" for first-time visitors
  (capital + ancient capital + modern metropolis). ~2,600 km one-way B→X→S.
  https://www.travelchinaguide.com/cityguides/golden-triangle-cities.htm
- Xi'an = home of the Terracotta Army (the "buried army"); Beijing = Great Wall + Forbidden City.
- Booking window: high-speed rail tickets open ~15 days ahead; book early for popular runs.
  https://www.travelchinaguide.com/china-trains/beijing-xian-highspeed.htm
