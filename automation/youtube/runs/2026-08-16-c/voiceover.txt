108 lakes in one valley — and no two the same color. Turquoise, jade, deep blue, all real, no filter. The secret is hiding on the lake floor. White travertine, calcium hardened out of the rock. The water is so clear you can see 30 meters down. Sunlight and algae paint the rest. Alpine lakes stacked from 2,000 to 3,400 meters. A world heritage home to wild pandas. Ride the shuttle to the top first, then walk the boardwalks downhill all day. 108 lakes, every one a different color.
