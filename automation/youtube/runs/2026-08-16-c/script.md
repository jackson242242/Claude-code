## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Jiuzhaigou: a Y-shaped alpine valley in northern Sichuan, ~108 turquoise lakes at 2,000–3,400 m.
- Lake colors come from suspended calcium/bicarbonate + travertine deposits (white lake floor), very clear low-turbidity water (visibility 30 m+ in places), multi-colored algae, seasonal vegetation, and the scattering/reflection/absorption of sunlight.
- Bedrock is carbonate limestone; dissolved calcium carbonate re-deposits as pale travertine that both dams and lines each lake.
- UNESCO Natural World Heritage Site (1992); habitat for giant panda and Sichuan takin.
