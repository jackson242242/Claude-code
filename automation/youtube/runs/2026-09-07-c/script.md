## The single claim
"In Chengdu, one cup of tea buys you the whole day" — you aren't paying for leaves,
you're leasing a bamboo chair; endless refills, no bill to hurry you out.

## Facts (all sourced this run 2026-09-07)
- Chengdu has more teahouses than any city in China (10,000+); a gaiwan of tea runs
  about ¥10–¥50 with bottomless hot-water refills. [FISU; realchinatrip 2026; quietroutes]
- The classic set is the three-piece "gaiwan" — lid (gai), bowl (wan), saucer — and a
  "tea doctor" (掺茶师) tops up everyone's water; there is no per-table teapot and no
  rush to leave. [FISU tea culture Chengdu]
- People's Park (Heming Teahouse) is the best-known century-old bamboo-chair spot. [realchinatrip; quietroutes]
