## Verified facts (two sources each, this run 2026-10-07)
- **Mogao Grottoes, Dunhuang:** ~492 painted caves carved over roughly 1,000 years; peak-season (May 1–Nov 30) Category A ticket **¥238** (~$34) = round-trip shuttle + 2 digital films + guided tour of 8 open caves; off-peak ¥140. Daily visitor numbers are **capped** (A-ticket vs limited emergency B-ticket).
  - China Xian Tour: https://www.chinaxiantour.com/mogao-caves.html
  - Lonely Planet: https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/mogao-grottoes/1496278
- **Mingsha Shan (Echoing-Sand Mountain) & Crescent Lake:** peak-season (Apr 20–Oct 19) ticket **¥110** (~$15); low season ¥55. **Night visits need no extra ticket** — the desert sky is the draw after dark.
  - China Xian Tour: https://www.chinaxiantour.com/echoing-sand-mountain-mingsha-shan-and-crescent-lake.html
  - iTravelBlog: https://en.itravelblog.net/mingsha-mountain-crescent-spring/
- Both sit beside Dunhuang, Gansu, on the Silk Road. Today (Oct 7) is still peak season for both → current prices apply.
