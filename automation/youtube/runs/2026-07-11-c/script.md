## Facts & sources (verified this run, 2026-07-11)
- "**一盅两件**" (one pot of tea, two dim sum) is the guiding phrase of Cantonese morning
  tea; classics are **虾饺 (har gow), 烧卖 (siu mai), 叉烧包 (char siu bao)**. Yum cha is
  a **social gathering**, not just a meal (Qing-dynasty origins; 二厘馆 tea stalls).
  - https://www.guangzhoudaytrips.com/cantonese-morning-tea/
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yum_cha
- **Guangzhou's regulation on the protection and inheritance of morning tea culture took
  effect May 1, 2026 — the country's first legislation dedicated specifically to yum cha**,
  including a strict 24-hour freshness window for hand-made dim sum.
  - https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/30/travel/china-guangzhou-dim-sum-regulation-intl-hnk
  - https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017722
  - https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/330406/Guangzhou-enforces-24-hour-freshness-mandate-to-safeguard-traditional-dim-sum-culture
