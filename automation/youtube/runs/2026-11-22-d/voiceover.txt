Nine to nine. Six days a week. In China, that schedule has a name — 996.

Add it up: seventy-two hours. Almost two full-time jobs in one.

For years, it was the quiet rule at many tech firms. Work hard, or someone else will.

Then in 2021, China's top court called a seventy-two-hour week illegal. Overtime is capped near thirty-six hours a month.

One delivery driver, fired for refusing 996, sued and won about eleven hundred dollars.

So, honest question: the paycheck, or the hours back?
