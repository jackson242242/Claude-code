There's only one pottery UNESCO calls world heritage. It turns stone and plant ash into the color of jade. They call it plum green and misty blue. It was perfected 800 years ago, in the Southern Song. The recipe is passed down inside families. It's fired again and again, up to 1,310 degrees. One wrong degree, and the jade never appears. Green so deep it looks wet — but it's stone. In Longquan, Zhejiang, you can throw and glaze your own piece in a day. The only ceramic the world vowed to protect.
