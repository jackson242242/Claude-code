## Facts (verified this run)
- 141 protected sites, **50,000+ statues**; highest achievement of grotto art 9th–13th c. (UNESCO 1999, "masterpiece of human creative genius").
- Earliest carvings ~650 AD (early Tang); major work from late 9th c. (Wei Junjing, Beishan); zenith in the Song.
- Outstanding evidence of the **harmonious synthesis of Buddhism, Taoism and Confucianism**.
- Baodingshan holds the grandest tableaux incl. a large reclining (Nirvana) Buddha (>30m).
- Beishan ~5,000 statues, some of the oldest.
