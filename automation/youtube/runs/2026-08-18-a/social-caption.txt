China Travel Expert 🧭 | Over 50,000 Buddhas were carved by hand into one hillside — and it took more than 250 years.

The Dazu Rock Carvings in Chongqing spread across 141 sites. Begun in the Tang, perfected under the Song — and the only place where Buddhism, Taoism and Confucianism share the same cliffs. At Baodingshan a reclining Buddha stretches over 30m, with 800-year-old paint still on the stone. Expert tip: start at Baodingshan for the grandest carvings.
