## Verified facts (sources in description)
- Jingdezhen = China's "porcelain capital" for ~1,000 years (imperial kilns from Song/Yuan/Ming). [common, sourced]
- Traditional Jingdezhen porcelain uses ~70+ (classically "72") distinct hand processes; one firing slip
  can ruin a whole batch. [ceramicfactories / mudandleaves]
- Authentic hand-thrown, hand-painted pieces: small pieces start ~$120–150, complex work $400–600+;
  a "$35 handcrafted bowl" sits at industrial price points where real handwork can't exist profitably. [oriental-artisan / mudandleaves]
- Mass-produced pieces are machine-printed (transfer patterns), often finished quickly. [mudandleaves]
