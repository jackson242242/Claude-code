## ★ Expert Tip
Skip the crowded Kuanzhai main lane at midday; go weekday morning, then walk to the century-old Heming Teahouse (鹤鸣茶社) in People's Park for open-air gaiwan tea + 采耳.
- https://gowesternchina.com/attractions/heming-tea-house-peoples-park-chengdu
