Guangzhou's Chen Clan Ancestral Hall (finished 1894) might be the most decorated building in China: rooflines crammed with Foshan Shiwan ceramic opera figures, halls carved in wood, brick and stone. Built as a clan temple AND an imperial-exam academy.

Roof or carvings — which would you shoot first? 👇
