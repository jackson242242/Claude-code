Siguniang's peak is 6,250 metres — altitude hits tourists far lower.
This is Mount Siguniang — Sichuan's four snow sister peaks.
Almost nobody climbs the 6,250 metre main peak, Yaomei Feng.
Most people walk the valleys — and that's the catch.
The base town, Rilong, already sits at 3,200 metres.
Trek toward Dafeng and you camp near 4,300 metres.
Climb too fast and altitude sickness ends the trip.
Would you trek the high valley — or stay in the town below?
