## Claim (= title = cue 1)
Mount Siguniang's peak is 6,250 m, but the altitude catches tourists far lower —
in the valleys everyone thinks are an easy stroll.

## Facts verified THIS RUN (2 sources each)
- **Yaomei Feng (幺妹峰) = 6,250 m**, highest of the four "sisters"; most visitors
  never attempt it. Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siguniang · sichuantravelguide.com ·
  travelchinawith.me (Mount Siguniang).
- **Rilong, the base town, ≈ 3,200 m**; the Haizi valley / Dafeng base camp ≈ 4,300 m
  — so even the "walking" routes sit at serious altitude. Sources:
  tibetantrekking.com · sichuantravelguide.com (Siguniang travel guide).
- **Autumn (late Sept–Nov) is the best season** — clear skies, colour, less rain.
  Sources: sichuantravelguide.com · windhorsetour.com.
- Shuangqiao valley is a near-effortless bus tour (gentle altitude intro); Changping
  is a real trekking day; Dafeng (~5,025 m) is the "beginner" 5,000 m peak and needs
  a ¥300 permit. (sichuantravelguide.com)
