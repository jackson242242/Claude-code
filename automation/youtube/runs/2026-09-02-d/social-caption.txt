China Travel Expert 🧭 | Keep following the old Silk Road west across the Gobi and you hit one of China's strangest sights: the Zhangye Danxia hills, striped like a rainbow. The colors are geology — iron in the sandstone rusting over ~24 million years into bands of red, gold and blue. UNESCO Global Geopark since 2019.
