## Verified facts + sources (this run)
- Canglang Pavilion = oldest of Suzhou's classical gardens; Northern Song, Qingli era 1041–1048 AD.
- Double Corridor (复廊) >100 m; 108 lattice windows (漏窗), no two the same design (square, fan,
  peach, vase, leaf, ruyi, etc.).
- Classical Gardens of Suzhou inscribed on UNESCO World Heritage list in 2000.
