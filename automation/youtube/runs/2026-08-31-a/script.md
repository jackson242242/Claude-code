## Verified facts + sources
- Zhoucheng village, Dali, Yunnan; Bai ethnic tie-dyeing (白族扎染). — chinaxiantour, visityunnanchina
- Listed National Intangible Cultural Heritage by the State Council, 2006. — Baidu Baike (Bai Ethnic Tie-Dyeing), visityunnanchina
- Natural indigo fermented from Isatis tinctoria / banlan (板蓝根) root + Cangshan herbs; ~16 hand steps. — researchgate PDF, chinaxiantour
- Plant dye leaves a faint herbal scent; blue symbolises Bai "innocence". — visityunnanchina
