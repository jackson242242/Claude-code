## Facts (all sourced this run 2026-09-05)
- Han beacon fires (烽燧 / 烽火): by DAY, smoke signals ("sui"); by NIGHT, fire signals
  ("feng"). ~6 signal types codified (Han doc 塞上烽火品约 graded enemy alarms). [baike]
- A tower stood roughly every 5 km along the Han Wall; garrisoned by 5–10+ men under a
  Beacon Chief; smoke/fire visible up to ~15 km. [travelchinaguide]
- Dunhuang's Han Wall runs ~136 km (85 miles) and is dotted with 80+ beacon towers;
  built of rammed loess (like Jiayuguan in EP6). [travelchinaguide]
- Speed: Han "urgent military dispatch" horse couriers made ~300 li/day; the beacon
  relay was far faster — Tang "Feng Shi" regulations later required beacon smoke to
  travel ~2,000 li within a day-and-night. (Comparative claim = beacon far outran the
  horse; the "capital in a day" specific is NOT asserted — unsourced.) [search 2026-09-05]
