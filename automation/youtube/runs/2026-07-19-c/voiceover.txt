Most stinky tofu is white — Changsha's is black. The secret is one pot of brine. Shiitake, winter bamboo shoot and Liuyang fermented black beans steep together for two whole years; the longer it ferments, the blacker and stronger it smells. But the moment it hits hot oil, the stink flies away, leaving a charred-black crust and a snow-white, savory center. In 1958, Chairman Mao tasted it and said: smells foul, tastes wonderful. In Changsha the greeting is simple — eaten yet? Stinkier means tastier. Dare to eat?
