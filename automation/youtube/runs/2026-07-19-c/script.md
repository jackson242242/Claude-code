## Narrative (burned bilingual subtitles = the narration; NO voiceover)
起 — hook: unlike the usual white stinky tofu, Changsha's is black — the secret is the brine.
承 — the brine: mushroom, winter bamboo shoot, Liuyang fermented black beans, fermented ~two years; the longer it sits, the blacker and stronger it smells.
转 — the turn: once it hits the oil the stink flies off (3-甲基吲哚 volatilizes); charred-black crust, snow-white inside.
合 — loop: 1958 Chairman Mao's line at 火宫殿「闻起来臭，吃起来香」; Changsha's「恰了冇？」; on-screen either-or dare-to-eat question.

## Verified facts + sources (checked this run 2026-07-19)
- **Why black**: the brine (卤水) is black — from ingredients like black beans / long fermentation; tofu soaked in it takes the color. — 新浪 · yssp88 长沙臭豆腐为什么是黑色
- **Why "stinkier = tastier"**: brine of 香菇、鲜冬笋、浏阳豆豉 (曲酒) fermented ~1.5–2 years produces **3-甲基吲哚 (skatole)** = the main stink; after high-heat frying that compound largely **volatilizes**, leaving mostly aroma. — 华声在线 · 百度百科TA说 · voc.com.cn 非遗正Young
- **火宫殿臭豆腐** is a national-level 非物质文化遗产 (制作技艺); known for 外皮墨黑焦脆、内里白软味鲜. — 中国非遗网 ihchina.cn · 湖南省政府门户
- **1958**: Chairman Mao visited 火宫殿, tasted the stinky tofu and said「长沙火宫殿的臭豆腐，闻起来臭，吃起来香」(widely cited culinary anecdote). — 华声在线 · 当代中国 ourchinastory
- **恰饭 / 恰了冇** — 西南官话（含长沙）把「吃」念作 qià；「恰了冇？」＝「吃了吗？」(dialect-bank 湖南/长沙, added 2026-07-16, verified 百度百科/维基·长沙话).
