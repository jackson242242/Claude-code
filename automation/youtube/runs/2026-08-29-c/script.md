## Facts + sources (verified this run)
- Tourists ride the metro with a transit QR in Alipay/WeChat (乘车码); scan to enter and exit, distance-based fare auto-deducted.

- Setup needs one-time passport real-name verification; easier on home wifi before arrival.
- Backup: single-journey token from a platform machine, or a physical cash transit card.
