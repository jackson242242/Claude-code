## Verified facts (sourced this run 2026-08-11)
- 劈丝 (splitting silk): a single silk thread is split into fractions before stitching; documented down to ~1/48 (and finer), each strand thinner than a human hair. Sources below.
- 双面绣 (double-sided embroidery): identical image on both faces, stitches hidden ("两面光"); the technique matured in the Song dynasty.
- 苏绣 is one of China's four famous embroideries, with roughly two thousand years of history.
