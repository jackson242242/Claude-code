A whole village, built in the shape of an ox. Not by accident — planned that way 900 years ago. A hill is the head, two old trees the horns. Canals wind through as the intestines. They carry fresh water to every doorstep. A crescent pond at the center is the stomach. 138 Ming and Qing houses still stand. A living village that flows like a painting. Come at dawn — the pond mirrors the whole village, no crowds. An ox-shaped village, alive 900 years.
