## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Yi County, Huangshan City, southern Anhui. Founded 1131 (Southern Song) by the Wang clan; 138 well-preserved Ming/Qing residences.
- UNESCO World Heritage 2000 (Ancient Villages in Southern Anhui — Xidi and Hongcun).
- Ox-shaped plan: Leigang Hill = head; two big trees on the hill = horns; houses = body; the winding stream = "intestines"; Moon Pond (crescent, center) = stomach; South Lake + four bridges = the legs. The system channels fresh water to every household and provides water for fire emergencies.
- Moon Pond was originally a spring, reshaped to supply daily water; South Lake was added ~200 years later when Moon Pond no longer sufficed.
- Nicknamed "the village in a Chinese painting."
