## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Yixing zisha teapots are unglazed; the clay has a "double-pore structure" (open + closed pores) that lets the pot breathe, preventing tea from stewing and smoothing bitterness → silkier soup. Clay rich in iron + quartz. — Seven Cups (sevencups.com), Path of Cha, Hidden Brews (hiddenbrews.com)
- Best clay from Huanglong Mountain (黄龙山 / Ben Shan), Yixing, Jiangsu. — Seven Cups / Path of Cha
- Record: a 1948 purple-clay teapot by master Gu Jingzhou (顾景舟) sold for ~US$2 million at China Guardian auction (Beijing, May 2010). — China Daily (chinadaily.com.cn), Teatrekker's Blog, TeaZen Essence
