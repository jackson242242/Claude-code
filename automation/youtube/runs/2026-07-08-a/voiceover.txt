Fifteen years. That's how long MIT has held the number-one spot on the QS World University Rankings — and the 2027 edition, released this June, keeps the streak alive.

But here's the wild part: MIT didn't just win. It posted a perfect overall score — a hundred out of a hundred.

So who's chasing them? For the first time, there's a tie for second: Imperial College London and Stanford, sharing the runner-up spot. Oxford slipped to fourth, and Harvard sits at fifth.

The full ranking covers more than fifteen hundred universities across a hundred and six education systems worldwide.

So the real question isn't who's number one — that hasn't changed in fifteen years. It's who finally breaks the streak. Follow for more on how the world's top colleges actually stack up.
