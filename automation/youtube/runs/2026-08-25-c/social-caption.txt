China Travel Expert 🧭 | Riding China's bullet trains as a foreigner is easier than it looks — and you never need to speak Chinese.

Every ticket is real-name, so your passport IS your ticket. Book on Trip.com (English + passport + foreign card) or the official 12306 app (now full English). Tickets open ~15 days out, you get an e-ticket, and you scan your passport straight onto the platform — no printing.

Expert Tip: the name you book under must match your boarding passport EXACTLY — China's real-name ticketing turns you away otherwise.
