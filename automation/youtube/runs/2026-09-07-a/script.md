## The single claim
"One wrong stroke ruins the entire scroll" — a real, verifiable paradox: on raw
xuan paper a corrected/retraced area dries darker than the original ink, so a line
cannot be touched up; each stroke must be one continuous movement.

## Facts (all sourced this run 2026-09-07)
- On (raw) xuan paper a corrected line shows darker once the ink dries, so it can't
  be "touched up"; this is why each stroke is done in one continuous movement.
  [art-virtue.com §7 paper/ink/stone]
- Xuan paper is prized for absorbing water/ink instantly, letting light and heavy
  strokes separate — ideal for calligraphy & ink painting. [china.org.cn; travelchinaguide]
