China Travel Expert 🧭 | Over 2,000 years ago, one Chinese city sent the world's first kites into the sky — and it's still the World Capital of Kites.

Weifang in Shandong has made kites for more than two millennia, peaking in the Ming dynasty, and was crowned Kite Capital in 1988. Every April, teams from dozens of countries fly here, and the city's signature is the giant dragon-head centipede kite. The craft has four skills: bind, paste, paint, fly. Expert tip: visit for the April festival and grab a flat-packing swallow kite.
