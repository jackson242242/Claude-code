## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Hotpot = communal simmering broth; diners cook their own raw ingredients in one shared boiling
  pot — a social ritual, not solo dining. [ChinaXianTour; Hospitality.Institute; G Adventures]
- Roots 2,000+ yrs (Zhou personal pots); communal broth form popularised ~Tang dynasty elite. [G Adventures]
- Modern spicy "mala" hotpot traced to riverside/dock workers in Sichuan/Chongqing, early 20th c.,
  boiling cheap cuts in spiced broth. [Wikipedia Chongqing hot pot; cenhot]
- Chongqing/Sichuan broth: dark-red, ~20 spices, chilies + mouth-numbing Sichuan pepper (麻/mala). [Wikipedia Chongqing hot pot]
