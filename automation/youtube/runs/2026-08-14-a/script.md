## Verified facts + sources (found this run)
- Canglang Pavilion (沧浪亭) has **108 non-identical 漏窗 (leaking windows)** — China Discovery "Changeful Windows in Suzhou Gardens" (blog.chinadiscovery.com/changeful-windows-in-suzhou-gardens/); Suzhou gardens hold "over a thousand" lattice styles.
- **漏窗 (lòuchuāng)** literally "leaking window" — the openwork lets the view "leak" through, framing the space beyond. — China Discovery.
- **移步换景** ("every step a new scene") — core classical garden design principle.
- **UNESCO World Heritage 1997** — four Suzhou gardens inscribed (Humble Administrator's, Lingering, Master of the Nets, Mountain Villa with Embracing Beauty), expanded 2000. — Wikipedia, Classical Gardens of Suzhou / Humble Administrator's Garden.
