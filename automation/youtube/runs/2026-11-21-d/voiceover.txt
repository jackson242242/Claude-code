A table for one. In China, that's not sad anymore — it's just a way to eat. The 2020 census counted about 125 million one-person households. That's one in four homes, up from one in seven a decade earlier. So restaurants adapted. The clearest sign is the one-person hotpot: your own simmering pot, your own broth, your own pace. No sharing, no small talk. Some shops even seat a toy bear across from you, so you're never quite alone. So tell me — a hotpot for one: is that freedom, or a little bit lonely?
