## Verified facts (WebSearch this run; "Verified September 2026")
- Foreigners can now use Alipay & WeChat Pay WITHOUT a Chinese bank account — register with
  passport + phone, link international Visa/Mastercard/JCB, verify via passport + live face
  scan; covers ~90% of situations.
- Foreign-card in-app limits ~¥3,000-6,500 per transaction (~$400-900), tens of thousands
  RMB/month; raised under PBOC directives through 2025-2026.
- Physical foreign cards: high-end hotels / international restaurants / airports / big shops
  only; not small local places or buses.
- Cash (RMB) is legal tender, must be accepted by law; ATMs at Bank of China / ICBC are the
  most reliably foreign-card-friendly.
- Trip.com accepts foreign Visa/MC (higher fees) vs Ctrip/Alipay (lower) — check final total.
