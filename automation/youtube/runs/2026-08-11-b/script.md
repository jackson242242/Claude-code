## Verified facts (sourced this run 2026-08-11)
- 《天工开物》(Ming, Song Yingxing) records porcelain making: "共计一坯之力，过手七十二，方克成器" — one piece passes through 72 hands before completion.
- The "72 processes" (七十二道工序) division-of-labor system formed in the Song dynasty; Jingdezhen has over a thousand years of hand-crafted porcelain history.
- The processes group into six workflows: practicing clay, throwing, refining, painting, glazing, firing — often called an early industrial division of labor.
- Taoxichuan (陶溪川) is a Jingdezhen creative district where returning young makers ("景漂") work by hand — a live, modern angle. (Kept as a general, verifiable pointer, not a claim about any specific person.)
