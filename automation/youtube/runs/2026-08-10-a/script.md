## Verified facts + sources (checked 2026-08-10)
- Silk-threaded jade burial suit (丝缕玉衣): 2,291 jade plaques laced with **red silk
  thread** — the **only silk-corded jade suit known in the world** (all others used gold,
  silver, or copper wire). ~173 cm; front-opening jacket (unlike northern royal suits).
- Unearthed **1983** from the tomb of **Zhao Mo (赵眜)**, the **second king of Nanyue**,
  on Jiefang North Rd, Guangzhou. Earliest complete jade suit found in China.
- Silk still holds its red colour; jade retains its luster. In situ at the **Museum of the
  Mausoleum of the Nanyue King (南越王博物院)**.
- Sources: Museum of the Mausoleum of the Nanyue King (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_the_Mausoleum_of_the_Nanyue_King);
  Jade burial suit (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_burial_suit); CGTN feature.
