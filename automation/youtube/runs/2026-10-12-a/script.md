## Verified facts (double-sourced this run, 2026-10-12)
- Zhangjiajie National Forest Park sits in Wulingyuan, ~30 km from the city; holds the quartz-sandstone pillars that inspired *Avatar*; tallest columns top 400 m; needs 2–3 days.
- Tianmen Mountain is a separate park near the city centre: glass skywalk, long cable car, 99-bend road. Its own ticket (~¥288); the Forest Park pass runs ~¥228–298 for multiple days. Tickets are NOT shared.
