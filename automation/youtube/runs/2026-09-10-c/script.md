## Verified facts + sources (found this run)
- Xiaolongbao = Shanghai soup dumpling; broth comes from a savory **aspic that sets like jelly and melts into soup when steamed** (not poured in).
- **18 folds/pleats** is the modern benchmark (Din Tai Fung's fixed standard; purists cite 18, some 14). Origin: **Nanxiang** (now Shanghai's Jiading district), **1870s**, refined by baker Huang Mingxian (thinner wrapper + aspic filling).
