## Verified facts (this run)
- A 4,000-year-old bowl of noodles was excavated at the Lajia site, Qinghai Province, China —
  the oldest empirical evidence of noodles anywhere in the world. Published in Nature (2005).
  [nationalgeographic.com; nature.com/articles/437967a]
- Preserved in an overturned, sealed earthenware bowl under ~3 m (10 ft) of sediment (the site
  was buried by an earthquake/flood). [nationalgeographic.com; Springer/Science Bulletin]
- Made from two millets indigenous to China (broomcorn + foxtail), not wheat. [nature.com; NatGeo]
- The find predates other early noodle claims (e.g., Italy), which is why it's cited as settling
  the "who invented noodles" debate in China's favor. [vagobond/NatGeo framing]
