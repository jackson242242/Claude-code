## Facts & sources (verified this run, 2026-07-11)
- Cormorant fishing on the Li River has a history of **more than 1,000 years**; unable
  to compete with modern fishing, it is now **largely practiced to entertain tourists**
  (evening shows in Guilin/Yangshuo; sunrise raft tours).
  - https://www.topchinatravel.com/china-activities/see-the-cormorant-fishing-show.htm
  - http://www.chinafacttours.com/guilin/attractions/cormorant-fishing.html
- The **back of the ¥20 RMB note** depicts the Li River scenery at **Xingping (兴坪)**,
  near Yangshuo (widely documented; a standard Guilin travel fact).
  - https://lirivercruises.com/li-river-fisherman/
- "**桂林山水甲天下**" ("Guilin's landscape is the finest under heaven") — classic
  praise line long associated with Guilin.
