## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Kumbum Monastery (Ta'er Si), near Lusar/湟中, Xining, Qinghai (Amdo). Founded/completed 1583 (construction began 1577 to commemorate Tsongkhapa 150 yrs after his death); birthplace of Tsongkhapa (b.1357), founder of the Gelug school. One of six great Gelug monasteries.
- Three Arts (三绝): Yak Butter Sculptures (酥油花), Barbola appliqué (堆绣), Murals. Butter sculptures shaped by monks; carved in cold / ice-chilled hands so they don't soften; seasonal displays.
- "Kumbum" = "100,000 enlightening bodies of the Buddha" (十万佛身), named for 100,000 Siṃhanāda images on the sacred sandalwood tree leaves.
