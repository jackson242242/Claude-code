## Verified facts (checked this run)
- 肠粉 origin is disputed — one account credits 泮塘荷仙馆 during the war years; another
  says street cries of 卖肠粉 were heard in late-Qing Guangzhou. Stated as "众说纷纭." —
  百度百科·肠粉 / 广州市政府·布拉肠
- 布拉肠 first made at 西关泮塘荷仙馆; plain (no filling) = 斋肠; later restaurants added
  shrimp/beef fillings. — 广州市政府门户·布拉肠
- Two main schools: 布拉肠 (cloth-pulled; batter often 粘米粉+澄面+粟粉+生粉, tasted for
  the filling) vs 抽屉式肠粉 (drawer-steamed; pure 米浆, tasted for noodle texture &
  sauce). — 知乎/广州市政府
- 1930s–40s 西关泮塘荷仙馆 added 油条/熟猪肉/熟虾仁 fillings; later 万昌茶楼 point-master
  spread white cloth on the copper rack, poured rice batter with fish/pork/beef → sheets
  "质白如雪、薄如纸." — 广州市政府门户
- Cheung fun is a headline item of 广式早茶 (yum cha). — 经济观察网/南都
- 粤语 wink: 唔该 (m4 goi1, please/thanks) + 得闲饮茶 (yum cha when free) — dialect-bank
  粤语, verified this run (腾讯新闻/百度百科/羊城网).
