## Claim (single/STAKES): You climb Mount Tai's ~6,600 steps through the night for a summit sunrise that the weather may hide.

## Verified facts (this run, 2+ sources)
- Jade Emperor Peak ~1,545 m (one source 1,532.7 m). [temples.org/temples/china/mount-tai; travelchina.org.cn]
- ~6,660 to 7,200 stone steps over a ~9.5 km route; traditional overnight climb in darkness (no views en route, temples closed at night); sunrise watched from the summit ~4 a.m. [thecrowdedplanet.com; travelchina.org.cn]
- "Sea of clouds" is the prized summit view; weather-dependent (slippery/icy in wet/winter; summer needs rain gear; spring & autumn best). [chinadaily.com.cn sea-of-clouds; thecrowdedplanet.com]
- A sacred peak climbed by emperors for ~2,000 years (one of the Five Great Mountains). [govt.chinadaily.com.cn mount-tai]
