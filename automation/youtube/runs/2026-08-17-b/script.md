## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Guangcai (Canton painted porcelain) developed by Guangzhou craftsmen in the Qing (from the Kangxi reign); gold polychrome overglaze enamel. [Wikipedia; Google Arts & Culture]
- Guangzhou was the sole legal port for export of Chinese goods to Europe before 1842. [Google Arts & Culture; THE VALUE]
- Blue-and-white / plain wares made at Jingdezhen; those to be enamelled were sent to Canton for painting. [Wikipedia]
- From the 1740s, European paintings / armorial designs sent to Guangzhou and reproduced onto porcelain. [Orientations via THE VALUE; Wikipedia]
- More than 60 million pieces of porcelain imported into Europe; peak in the last two decades of the 18th century. [THE VALUE]
