China Travel Expert 🧭 | 60 million pieces of porcelain left China through a single city.

Before 1842, Guangzhou was China's only legal export port. Plain white porcelain came from Jingdezhen; Canton workshops repainted it in gold and rose enamel. From the 1740s, European nobles mailed in their own crests and portraits for artisans to copy onto the glaze — this is Guangcai, Canton porcelain, made for the world.
