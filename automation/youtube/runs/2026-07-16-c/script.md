## Angle
"How many coffees does the average Chinese person drink a year?" The answer (22) feels
low — until you see the growth (9 → 22 in five years, +33% in one year) and the skew
(Shanghai ~2x national, tier-1/2 cities = 72% of consumption). A clean numbers Short
that closes by turning the question on the viewer.

## Verified facts (all this run)
- 2024 national per-capita coffee consumption: 22.24 cups/year, up 33% vs 2023. (新浪财经 / 上海交大 report, May 2025)
- 2020 → 2023: per-capita rose 9.1 → 16.74 cups; so "5 years ago ≈ 9 cups" is accurate. (走出去导航网)
- Tier-1 and tier-2 cities = 72% of total consumption; tier 3-6 = 28%. (2024 城市咖啡发展报告)
- Shanghai per-capita is roughly twice the national figure; Shanghai had 9,115 cafés in 2024. (报告 via 新浪财经)
- 2024 industry scale 3,133亿元 (+18.1%); 2025 total bean consumption ~400,000 tonnes. (北京新闻 / investgo)
