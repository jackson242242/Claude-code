China Travel Expert 🧭 | How famous is one tree? China's Guest-Greeting Pine has a full-time human guardian — and he's the 19th generation on the job.

The Yingke Song clings to a granite cliff on Huangshan (Yellow Mountain), Anhui — a UNESCO site of cloud seas and stone peaks. The pine is believed to be over 1,000 years old, branches reaching out like arms welcoming guests. Its keeper logs the temperature, humidity and every needle around the clock. Expert tip: ride the first cable car and beat the crowd to the cloud sea.
