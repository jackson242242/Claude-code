It looks like a nine-story pagoda. Step inside and you'll count seventeen. Hidden mezzanine floors double almost every level. It has stood 900 years — the oldest landmark in Guangzhou. A passing poet, Su Dongpo, saw six banyan trees at the gate and named the temple "Six Banyans." Locals still slip in to light incense. Its colorful painted eaves earned it the name "Flower Pagoda." Expert tip: climb it right when the gate opens — the narrow stairs jam by mid-morning. Nine floors or seventeen — how many would you count?
