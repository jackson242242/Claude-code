From the front it's a tower. Step to the side and it's a pagoda. For 600 years this iron-red giant has watched over Guangzhou. Built in 1380, it was once the city's tallest, five tiers stacked and narrowing, with red walls and green tiles. It was destroyed and rebuilt five times over. Locals still climb the hill to see it. They call it the finest view in the south. Expert tip: it's now the Guangzhou Museum — climb up for the view over the old city. A tower, or a pagoda — which do you see?
