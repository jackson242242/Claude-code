China Travel Expert 🧭 | From the front it's a tower — from the side, a pagoda. And it's guarded Guangzhou for 600 years.

Zhenhai Tower (越秀山镇海楼) was built in 1380 and was once the tallest thing in the city — iron-red walls, five narrowing tiers, "the finest view in the south." Destroyed and rebuilt five times, it now houses the Guangzhou Museum. Climb up for the old-city view.
