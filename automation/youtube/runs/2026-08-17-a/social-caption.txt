China Travel Expert 🧭 | This Guangzhou pagoda looks 9 floors tall — step inside and there are 17.

Guangzhou's Flower Pagoda (Temple of the Six Banyan Trees) was built in 1097. Hidden mezzanine floors sit between each visible level, so 9 outside becomes 17 inside. The Song poet Su Dongpo named the temple after six banyan trees at its gate. Climb it at opening — the narrow stairs jam by mid-morning.
