## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Flower Pagoda built 1097, 57.6m, octagonal; tallest & oldest surviving landmark in Guangzhou; 9 exterior levels but hidden mezzanines → 17 internal layers. [ChinaDiscovery; TopChinaTravel; hpcbristol]
- Temple of Six Banyan Trees (Liurong Temple) originally built AD 537 (~1500y); one of the early homes of Chinese Chan (Zen) Buddhism. [ChinaDiscovery; TopChinaTravel]
- Su Dongpo (Su Shi), Northern Song poet, c.1100 saw six banyan trees at the gate, wrote "Liu Rong" (Six Banyans); temple renamed. [ChinaDiscovery; TopChinaTravel]
- "Flower Pagoda" (花塔) named for its colorful painted exterior. [ChinaDiscovery; TopChinaTravel]
