## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Built 1380 (early Ming) by marquis Zhu Liangzu, on the hill in today's Yuexiu Park, Guangzhou. [Wikipedia; TravelChinaGuide]
- ~28m tall, 31m wide; five tiers each smaller than the last; iron-red walls, green tiles, flying eaves; "tower from the front, pagoda from the side". [Wikipedia; TravelChinaGuide]
- Tallest building in Guangzhou when built; reputed "the finest view in the south" (岭南第一胜览). [TravelChinaGuide; newsgd]
- Destroyed and rebuilt five times; changed from wood to reinforced concrete in 1928. [Wikipedia]
- Now houses the Guangzhou Museum (since the late 1920s). [TravelChinaGuide; Wikipedia; newsgd]
