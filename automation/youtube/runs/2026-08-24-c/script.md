## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- The Song-dynasty "Pingjiang Map" (平江图), drawn 1229, charts Pingjiang Road as a main
  thoroughfare of old (east) Suzhou. — CGTN, TravelChinaGuide.
- For 800+ years the lane has kept its river-and-road parallel design and low
  whitewashed architecture. — CGTN, Nanjinger.
- Pingjiang River is a tributary of the Grand Canal; the canal water made it a
  commercial hub. — TravelChinaGuide, Dong's Journey.
- The lane is lined with teahouses hosting Kunqu opera and Suzhou Pingtan storytelling;
  it sits in the UNESCO Grand Canal / Classical Gardens context and won a UNESCO
  Asia-Pacific Heritage honorable mention (2005). — CGTN, Nanjinger.
