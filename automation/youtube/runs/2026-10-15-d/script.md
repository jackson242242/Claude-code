## Claim / facts (2-source verified this run)
- **Shaxi** is "probably the most intact horse caravan town on the ancient tea route" (Wikipedia), sitting between Dali and Lijiang — about **100 km (~3 hrs by bus) south of Lijiang**.
- It was a trading stop on the **~1,000-year-old Tea-Horse Road** (Tang–Song origin), where caravans swapped Yunnan tea for Tibetan horses; peak in Ming–Qing.
- **The last caravans passed through in 1949**, after which the town fell into isolation ("time stands still"). WMF listed its market square among the 100 most endangered sites (2001); ETH Zürich–led restoration from 2006.
- **Sideng Friday market**: every Friday the old square still trades — Bai and Yi villagers bring produce, supplies, even horses. → Expert Tip.
