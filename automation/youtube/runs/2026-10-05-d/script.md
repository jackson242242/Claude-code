## Verified facts (this run)
- Qinghai-Gansu grand loop ("大环线") ≈ 2,800 km, typically sold as a 6–8 day self-drive.
- Classic 7-day ring: Xining → Qinghai Lake → Chaka Salt Lake → Dachaidan/Emerald Lake → Yadan/315 U-highway → Dunhuang (Mogao Caves + Mingsha dunes & Crescent Lake) → Jiayuguan → Zhangye Danxia → Qilian grassland → Menyuan → Xining.
- Pace: roughly 5 hours of driving per day on the 7-day version.
- Mogao Caves require tickets booked online ~1 month in advance.
