## Verified facts (this run)
- The Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge is a 55 km bridge-island-tunnel system across the
  Lingdingyang channel of the Pearl River estuary, linking Hong Kong with Zhuhai and Macau —
  the world's longest sea crossing. [ICE (ice.org.uk); topchinatravel.com]
- Main bridge section ~29.6 km; includes a 6.7 km offshore immersed tunnel (undersea section
  ~5.6 km) — the longest/deepest immersed-tube tunnel, max depth ~50 m — so ships can pass above.
  [sciencedirect.com; baike.baidu.com; nomadicnotes.com]
- Two artificial islands connect the bridge spans to the undersea tunnel. [sciencedirect; nomadicnotes]
- Private cross-border driving is heavily restricted; visitors normally use the cross-border
  shuttle bus between the HK and Zhuhai/Macau ports. [general travel guidance — expert tip]
