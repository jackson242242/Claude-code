China Travel Expert 🧭 | A desert lake that refuses to dry up.

Crescent Lake near Dunhuang loses ~2,400mm to evaporation a year and gets ~50mm of rain — yet survives inside the Singing Sand dunes. Silk Road caravans rested here. Climb the dunes at sunrise.
