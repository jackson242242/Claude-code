## Verified facts + sources
- Crescent Lake (Yueyaquan), crescent-moon oasis 6km south of Dunhuang, Gansu, amid Mingsha (Singing Sand) dunes. — wikipedia, chinadiscovery
- Annual evaporation ~2,400mm vs ~50mm precipitation, yet survives centuries; "First Spring in the Deserts." — visitourchina, travelchinaguide
- Mingsha dunes ~40km × 20km, highest ~250m; one of China's Four Great Singing Sands. — chinadiscovery
- One of the Eight Great Landscapes of Dunhuang since Western Han (206BC–24AD); on the Silk Road. — travelchinaguide, wikipedia
