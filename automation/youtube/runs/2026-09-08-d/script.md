**Verified facts (this-run WebSearch):**
- Qinghai–Tibet Railway = world's highest & longest plateau railway; Xining → Lhasa.
- Highest point Tanggula Pass 5,072 m; Tanggula station 5,068 m = world's highest.
- Enclosed carriages use a diffusion oxygen-supply system raising cabin oxygen to
  ~23.5%, with personal oxygen ports at each seat to prevent altitude sickness.
- Xining→Lhasa ≈ a full day and night (~21 h). At ~5,000 m the air holds roughly half
  the oxygen of sea level (partial-pressure ≈ 50%) — stated as "about half."
- Foreign travelers need a Tibet Travel Permit (Tibet Entry Permit), applied for only
  by authorized/licensed travel agencies, on a pre-arranged guided tour.
