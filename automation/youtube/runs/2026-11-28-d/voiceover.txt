Here's a quiet shift in China you won't see on a postcard.

In the country's big cities, the most popular pet is no longer the dog. It's the cat.

One industry survey counted about seventy-one million pet cats in urban China — against fifty-two million dogs.

Why? Ask the young people. In a small apartment, after a twelve-hour day, a cat is quiet, cheap, and doesn't need a walk at midnight.

And here's the number that stopped me. By one bank's estimate, China's city pets now outnumber its children under four.

A nation of cat people — lonely, or just practical? Tell me below.
