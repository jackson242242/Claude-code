## Verified facts + sources (checked 2026-08-10)
- **Wall:** ~6 km circuit, **6 gates + 72 watchtowers**, built ~**1370** (Ming, Hongwu era);
  a key reason for UNESCO listing. Best-preserved Ming-Qing walled county town; city has
  2,700+ years of history and keeps its authentic layout.
- **Rishengchang (日升昌):** established **1823**, China's **first 票号 (draft bank)** and the
  birthplace of Chinese modern banking; at its height it handled close to half of China's
  silver trade ("汇通天下").
- **UNESCO World Heritage:** inscribed **1997** (with Shuanglin & Zhenguo temples).
- Sources: History Hit (Pingyao Ming City Walls); UNESCO WHC #812; CGTN Pingyao feature.
