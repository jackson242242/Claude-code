## Verified facts + sources (this run, WebSearch)
1. **Threshold 门槛:** stepping on the raised wooden threshold is disrespectful / draws
   disapproving looks; the threshold is traditionally meant to keep bad influences out.
2. **Doors:** enter the main hall by the RIGHT door (dragon gate), exit LEFT (tiger gate);
   the CENTER door is reserved for the gods — visitors should not use it.
3. **Incense:** offer THREE sticks (Buddha/Dharma/Sangha) or a single stick; NEVER four
   (四 sounds like 死, "death"); some deities receive 5 or 7. Insert with both hands.
4. **Respectful practice:** three sticks, both hands, raised to forehead level, three bows.
