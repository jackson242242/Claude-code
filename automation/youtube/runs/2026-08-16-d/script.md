## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Qinghai Lake: China's largest inland saltwater (saline) lake; area > 4,600 km²; average depth ~18 m; shoreline ~360 km.
- Surface elevation ~3,200–3,260 m — the highest-altitude lake among the world's 50 largest.
- ~150 km from Xining, Qinghai province; on the northeastern Qinghai–Tibet Plateau. Nicknamed "the Most Beautiful Lake in China."
- Bird Islands (Cormorant Island, Egg Island) on the western peninsula have been part of the Qinghai Lake nature protection zone / bird sanctuary since 1997.
