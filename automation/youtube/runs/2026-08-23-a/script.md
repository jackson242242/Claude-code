## Verified facts (WebSearch this run)

- 永字八法 (Eight Principles of Yong): the single character 永 ("eternity") contains all eight fundamental brush strokes, the building blocks of thousands of characters. [UNESCO; china.org.cn]
- Practiced 2,000+ years; uses the Four Treasures of the Study 文房四宝 — brush, inkstick, paper, inkstone. [UNESCO; ebsco]
