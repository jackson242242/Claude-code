## Verified facts + sources (found THIS run)
- Longquan celadon has a ~1,700-year history (from the 3rd century); prized for a jade-like
  green glaze. UNESCO inscribed its traditional firing techniques on the Intangible Cultural
  Heritage list in 2009. Fired in long "dragon kilns"; firing range ~1,180–1,300°C, with the
  higher range giving the best greens/blues. Green develops in a reduction (low-oxygen)
  atmosphere from iron in the glaze; an oxidising atmosphere shifts the colour toward
  brown/yellow.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longquan_celadon
  - https://www.china.org.cn/arts/2023-12/29/content_116903996.htm (UNESCO 2009, dragon kilns)
