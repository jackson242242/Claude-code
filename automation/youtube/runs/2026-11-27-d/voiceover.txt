In 2021, a man who had already made a fortune in phones did something strange. He bet it all again.

His name is Lei Jun, the founder of Xiaomi — that's xiǎomǐ, and it means "little rice." He called building a car the last big project of his life, and promised up to ten billion dollars of the company's own money.

Everyone said he was too late. Tesla was years ahead.

Then, in 2024, the car finally launched... and sold nearly ninety thousand in a single day.

So — would you bet your whole legacy on one last idea... or quit while you're winning?
