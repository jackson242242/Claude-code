## Verified facts (sources this run)
- Yumen Pass ("Jade Gate") ~80 km NW of Dunhuang, Gansu. — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yumen_Pass; chinahighlights
- Erected by Emperor Wu of Han soon after 121 BCE; checkpoint on the Hexi Corridor; northern Silk Road route; traded jade & horses with Central Asia; named for jade caravans. — wikipedia; CGTN
- Built of rammed earth; today only gatehouse, a beacon tower and a portion of the Han (tamped-earth) Great Wall remain. — asiaculturaltravel; wikipedia
- Inscribed 2014 on the UNESCO World Heritage List (Silk Roads). — wikipedia; chinahighlights
