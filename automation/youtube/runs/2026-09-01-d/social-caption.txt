China Travel Expert 🧭 | A mud wall in the Gobi once guarded all of China. This is Yumen Pass — the "Jade Gate" — built by Emperor Wu around 121 BC. Every Silk Road caravan squeezed through here; today only rammed earth and a beacon tower remain.
Expert Tip: pair Yumen with Dunhuang's Mogao Caves in one trip.
