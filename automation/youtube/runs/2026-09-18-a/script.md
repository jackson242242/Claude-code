# 2026-09-18 slot a — Shanghai: skip the cruise, take the 2-yuan ferry
Pillar: china-culture (Friday Expert Opinions) · Format: Short 9:16 · Hook family: PRICE-ANCHOR (Expert Opinion / tourist-mistake)

## Single claim
A Huangpu River sightseeing cruise costs ~120 yuan (~$17); the local commuter ferry crosses the same river for 2 yuan (~$0.30) — same Bund + Pudong skyline.

## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Tourist Huangpu sightseeing cruise: ~120–130 RMB. (China Discovery)
- Local pedestrian ferry crossing: 2 RMB; ~10 min crossing, every ~15 min; both skylines in view. (TravelChinaGuide; China Wonders Guide)
- ~60x price gap; ~$17 vs ~$0.30.
