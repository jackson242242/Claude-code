## Claim & facts (verified this run)
- The rarest Da Hong Pao (mother-tree leaves) is valued at roughly **US$1.2 million per kilogram** (2025 valuation). [momenti]
- The original tea comes from **six mother trees**, hundreds of years old, on a cliff in the **Wuyi Mountains, Fujian**. Total annual yield from the six trees is under 1 kg. [teasenz / teadaocultivation]
- In **1998, 20 grams sold for ¥156,800** at auction (~¥7,840/gram). [momenti]
- Harvesting the mother trees has been **banned since 2006** to protect them. [search consensus]
- Honest note: commercial "Da Hong Pao" oolong (same Wuyi rock-tea style, not mother-tree leaves) is inexpensive and widely sold — that is what a traveler actually drinks.
