Seven in ten old Shanghainese lived here. Behind one stone gate — shikumen. These lanes are called longtang. Laundry poles turn the sky to a canopy. Breakfast stalls fire up before dawn. Neighbors gossip on stools. Built in the 1850s: Western frame, Chinese heart. This is everyday Shanghai, not the skyline. Expert Tip: come at 7am, the lane wakes with breakfast, not tourists.
