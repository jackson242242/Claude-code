## Verified facts (this run)
- "Spoon-tendon" / 匙柄 (a chuck cut) is the finest cut, <1% of a whole cow.
- A menu lists up to ~10 different beef cuts; one cow yields limited hotpot cuts.
- Precise dip times: 吊龙 8–10s, 五花趾 ~8s, 匙柄 ~7s, tenderloin ~5s,
  胸口朥 (Xiong Kou Lao) needs 3+ minutes. General guidance ~8s/slice.
- Chaoshan beef is fresh/never-frozen, hand-sliced the same day; clear broth,
  dip clear broth then sha-cha sauce.
