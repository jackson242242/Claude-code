## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- Location: Hunyuan County, Datong, Shanxi. Built late Northern Wei, ~491 AD → 1,500+ years old.
- 75 m / 246 ft above the ground on a sheer cliff.
- Supported by ~27 wooden crossbeams inserted into the rock + iron reinforcement; 40 halls/pavilions. Visible stilts are largely decorative.
- Only temple in China dedicated to three faiths at once: Buddhism, Taoism, Confucianism.
- Built high partly to escape flooding from the valley below.
