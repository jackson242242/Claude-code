## Facts + sources (verified this run)
- All Chinese tea comes from one plant (Camellia sinensis); the six categories (green, yellow, dark, white, oolong, black) differ mainly by processing / oxidation level. Green = barely oxidized; black = fully oxidized; oolong = partial. Six base categories → over 2,000 tea products. — UNESCO ICH 01884; Global Times 2022-11.
- UNESCO inscribed "Traditional tea processing techniques and associated social practices in China" on the Representative List in 2022 (Nov 29, 2022). — https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-tea-processing-techniques-and-associated-social-practices-in-china-01884
- Inscription involves 44 national tea-related ICH items across 15 provinces/regions. — Global Times / CGTN 2022-11.
- Teahouse custom: first steep often used to rinse/wake the leaves (Expert Tip). — general tea practice (gongfu cha).
