## Verified facts + sources
- Dongshankou: 600+ Republican-era (民国) red-brick colonial villas; former enclave of officials & returned overseas-Chinese; one of Guangzhou's first historic-culture protection areas — 界面新闻; 百度百科.
- Now dense with independent cafés in the villas (急急脚/Captain/Sigma etc.) — Zhujianger; 界面新闻.
- 「东山少爷，西关小姐」: classic Guangzhou saying — Dongshan = wealthy young masters, Xiguan = merchant-district young ladies. Widely documented.
- 「得闲饮咖啡」: Cantonese, adapted from 得闲饮茶 ("come for tea when you're free") — dialect-bank 粤语 (饮咖啡 form), clean.
