China Travel Expert 🧭 | Where the Great Wall actually ends — alone in the desert

Everyone shoots the Wall on its green mountains near Beijing. Follow it west and it runs out of mountains, ending on the edge of the Gobi at Jiayuguan — the Ming Wall's westernmost fort, begun in 1372, built not of brick but of rammed desert loess. In 1873 it was named "the First and Greatest Pass Under Heaven." West of its gate: nothing but the road every Silk Road caravan had to take.

Tip: Jiayuguan has its own bullet-train stop — come at sunset when the walls glow gold.
