China Travel Expert 🧭 | In Guangzhou, "drinking tea" is really a 3-hour feast

Cantonese yum cha literally means "drink tea" — but the tea is the smallest part. It's an endless parade of dim sum (点心, "touch the heart"): steamed dumplings, buns, rice rolls, egg tarts, once wheeled to your table on carts. The classic order is one pot of tea + two dishes — nobody stops at two.

Tip: go before 10am; start with har gow and char siu bao.
