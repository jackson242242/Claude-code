## Verified facts (WebSearch this run)
- 点心 dim sum = "touch the heart." Cantonese yum cha ("drink tea") = morning-tea
  tradition, originated in the Qing dynasty and developed rapidly in Guangzhou in the
  late 19th century. Classic order 一盅两件 = one pot of tea + two dishes. Traditionally
  served from push carts (or neck trays); order-by-slip now common. Sources:
  - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42779-023-00180-9 (Journal of Ethnic Foods)
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yum_cha
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dim_sum
