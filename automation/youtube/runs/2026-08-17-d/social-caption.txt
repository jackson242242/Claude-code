China Travel Expert 🧭 | A lake inches deep that mirrors the entire sky — the Mirror of the Sky.

Chaka Salt Lake (~3,100m, Qinghai) is stop 3 on the Qinghai-Gansu Loop, ~150km from Qinghai Lake. In summer the water lies just inches over a near-pure salt crust, turning the surface into a perfect mirror. Come before 9am — by noon the wind breaks the glass.
