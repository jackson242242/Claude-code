## Verified facts (sources found this run)
- ~3,100m elevation, eastern Qinghai; ~105 km² oval; ~150km SW of Qinghai Lake. [Asia Odyssey; ChinaDiscovery]
- Tibetan "Chaka" = salt lake; nicknamed "Mirror of the Sky." [Asia Odyssey; China Xian Tour]
- Shallow (inches in summer) over a hard, near-pure halite salt crust → natural mirror. [ChinaDiscovery; Asia Odyssey]
- China's first green edible-salt production base. [China Xian Tour]
- Best reflection early morning / late afternoon, low wind. [ChinaDiscovery]
