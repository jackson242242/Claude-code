A lake so shallow you can walk across it — and so still it mirrors the entire sky. This is stop three on the Qinghai-Gansu Loop, 150km on from Qinghai Lake. Under your feet is a crust of near-pure salt. They call it the Mirror of the Sky. At 3,100 meters, the dawn wind falls still, and that's when the reflection turns perfect. It's also China's first green edible-salt base. Reward stop: a hot bowl by the roadside before you ride on. Expert tip: arrive before 9am — by noon the wind ripples the mirror.
