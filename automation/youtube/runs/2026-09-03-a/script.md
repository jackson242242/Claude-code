## Verified facts (WebSearch this run)
- Yixing zisha = "purple sand" clay, iron/quartz/mica-rich, found around Yixing
  (Dingshu), Jiangsu — and almost nowhere else. Unglazed; highly porous "double-pore"
  structure absorbs tea oils, develops patina, "seasons" → rounder/richer taste over
  time; hence no soap. ~600-year craft since the Ming dynasty. Listed as one of China's
  national intangible cultural heritages (2006 national list). Sources:
  - https://yixingteapot.art/blogs/help-center/zisha-teapot
  - https://sevencups.com/learn-about-tea/brewing-tea/making-yixing-teaware/
  - https://teajourney.pub/yixing-zisha-teapot/
- NOTE: some commercial blogs claim a UNESCO listing; not corroborated by an official
  source, so the video/description say "China's national intangible cultural heritage"
  only (no UNESCO claim).
