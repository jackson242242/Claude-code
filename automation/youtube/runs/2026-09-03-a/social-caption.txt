China Travel Expert 🧭 | The Chinese teapot that gets better the more you use it

Yixing zisha (purple-clay) pots come from one town in Jiangsu — the clay is found almost nowhere else. Left unglazed on purpose, the porous clay drinks in your tea's oils and "seasons" over years, so every cup tastes rounder. That's why you never wash it with soap. A 600-year-old craft, still done by hand.

Tip: match one pot to one tea, rinse with hot water only.
