## Angle
The name puzzle as the hook: why 肉夹馍 ("meat-in-bun") and not the grammatically
"correct" 馍夹肉 ("bun-holds-meat")? The reveal — in Xi'an dialect 馍夹肉 sounds like
没夹肉 ("no meat added") — is the curiosity payoff that loops the Short. Along the way,
the craft: 白吉馍 crust, 腊汁肉 slow-braised in aged broth. Shaanxi wink: 嫽扎咧 (great!).

## Factual claims + sources (verified this run 2026-07-12)
- 肉夹馍 = 古汉语 "肉夹于馍" 的简称 (meat clasped in the bun); the fronting of 肉 keeps
  the emphasis on the meat. In Xi'an speech 馍夹肉 is easily heard as 没夹肉 ("no meat").
  — 肉夹馍 百度百科: https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E8%82%89%E5%A4%B9%E9%A6%8D/370129
  — 肉夹馍 维基百科: https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%82%89%E5%A4%B9%E9%A6%8D
- 腊汁肉: 选猪硬肋条肉，多种中草药入老汤，文火慢炖; 战国时称"寒肉"; 2016-01-08 入选陕西省
  非物质文化遗产。— 腊汁肉夹馍 百度百科: https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E8%85%8A%E6%B1%81%E8%82%89%E5%A4%B9%E9%A6%8D/657995
- 白吉馍 "铁圈虎背菊花心" 饼面特征。— 西安旅游网: https://m.drtjg.com/news/?7997.html
- 陕西话 嫽扎咧 (liáo zā lie) = 好极了/棒极了 (clean, iconic Shaanxi phrase).
