## Verified facts (2 sources each, this run)
- Jingdezhen's ceramics history traces back **>1,700 years**; named "Jingdezhen" by Emperor Zhenzong in the Song **Jingde** reign (1004); **Imperial Kiln Factory** established **1369** (Ming, Hongwu), producing exclusively for the court. — Britannica; China Federation of Literary and Art Circles (cflac); Wikipedia *Jingdezhen porcelain*; CGTN.
- Classic descriptor of the ware: **"白如玉，薄如纸，声如磬，明如镜"** (white as jade, thin as paper, sound like a chime, bright as a mirror) — traditional saying (framed as "the old saying").
- **72 processes** ("过手七十二，方克成器", 宋应星《天工开物》) — framed as "by the classic count."
