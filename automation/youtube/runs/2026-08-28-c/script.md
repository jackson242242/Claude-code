## Verified facts + sources (this run, WebSearch)
- 9 Classical Gardens of Suzhou inscribed as UNESCO World Heritage (1997, expanded 2000).
- Design philosophy: recreate nature in miniature — hills (rockery), water, plants, pavilions — a
  whole 山水 landscape folded into a walled courtyard; scholar-official retreats (Song–Qing).
- 借景 "borrowed scenery": distant pagodas / hills framed into the view to extend space.
- Winding paths + latticed windows → "changing scenery with every step" (步移景异); the layout is
  enclosed yet feels open, and you can't see the whole garden at once (by design).
- Humble Administrator's Garden (拙政园) is the largest and most celebrated; Lingering Garden is
  famed for calligraphy-inlaid halls.
- 成语/idiom wink (中式 checklist): 曲径通幽 + 步移景异.
