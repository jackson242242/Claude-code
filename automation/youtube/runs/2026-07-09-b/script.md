## Verified facts + sources (checked this run, 2026-07-09)
- From **29 July 2026**, Singapore's **Higher Education Student Loan (HESL)** replaces the MOE
  Tuition Fee Loan, Study Loan, and Overseas Student Programme Loan; it can cover **up to 100%
  of subsidised tuition** (base + means-tested balance components) plus a living allowance.
  Interest-free during the course; interest begins on graduation/leaving; max **10-year**
  repayment; minimum **S$100/month**; SLSG portal opens 10am, 29 July 2026.
  — MOE (moe.gov.sg) "Worried About the Cost of Higher Education in Singapore? Read This";
    Singapore Institute of Technology HESL page.
  https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/edtalks/worried-about-the-cost-of-higher-education-in-singapore-read-this
  https://www.singaporetech.edu.sg/admissions/financial-aid/loans/higher-student-loan-hesl-scheme
- US context: private nonprofit tuition averaged ~$45,000 in 2025-26 (up ~4%), and "free
  tuition" remains a policy debate. — SoFi / College Board figures cited this run.
  https://www.sofi.com/learn/content/increasing-college-tuition/
