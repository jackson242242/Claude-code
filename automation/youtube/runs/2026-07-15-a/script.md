## Verified facts (sourced this run)
- Washed (水洗) = fermented + washed → clean flavor, bright acidity, mild sweetness.
  Natural / sun-dried (日晒) = bean dried inside the cherry → bolder, fuller body,
  more pronounced syrupy sweetness and fruitiness. (Homegrounds; Sweet Maria's)
- Yunnan-specific: washed Yunnan Catimor = clean acidity, winey/fruity, strong
  sweetness; natural = black tea, plum, fudge notes. (Burman Coffee; Homegrounds)
- Yunnan grows >95% of China's coffee; honey (蜜处理) is a third method. (Sweet Maria's)
