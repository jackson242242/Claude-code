## Verified facts (double-sourced this run)
- Mogao Caves ("Thousand Buddha Grottoes"), ~25 km SE of Dunhuang, Gansu; ~500 surviving
  decorated caves cut into a cliff on the Silk Road; UNESCO World Heritage.
- Library Cave (Cave 17): sealed for nearly 900 years; ~50,000 manuscripts, scrolls,
  booklets and silk/paper paintings in 17 languages; rediscovered in 1900 by Daoist priest
  Wang Yuanlu. Significance compared to Tutankhamun's tomb.
  - The Art Newspaper (2024) — Mogao manuscripts / world's oldest printed book
  - Wikipedia — Dunhuang manuscripts (Cave 17, ~50,000 items, 17 languages, sealed ~11th c.)
- Expert Tip fact: the Dunhuang Academy caps and times daily visitors to Mogao; advance
  booking is required in peak season (well-documented conservation measure).
