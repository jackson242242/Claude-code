You'll hear the Forbidden City has 9,999 rooms — but that's folklore, a lucky number. Every real survey counts fewer: about 8,886 bays across roughly 980 buildings. The catch is the unit — a "bay" is the space between four pillars, not quite a room. Is a bay a room? Tell me I'm wrong. 🧭 #ChinaTravel #ChinaTravelExpert
