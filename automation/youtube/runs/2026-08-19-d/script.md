## Verified facts (2+ sources)
- Dafo Temple, Zhangye, Gansu; built 1098, Western Xia dynasty; largest & best-preserved Western Xia imperial temple. [Wikipedia; TravelChinaGuide]
- Reclining Buddha 34.5m long, shoulders 7.5m, ears ~4m, feet 5.2m; Asia's largest reclining clay Buddha. [ChinaHighlights; TravelChinaGuide]
- Built on a wooden armature, modeled in clay + straw, gilded; hollow interior. [ChinaDiscovery; Wikipedia]
