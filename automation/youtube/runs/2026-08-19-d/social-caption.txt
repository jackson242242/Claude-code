China Travel Expert 🧭 | Stop six on the Qinghai-Gansu loop hides Asia's largest reclining Buddha made of clay — 34.5 meters long.

Zhangye's Great Buddha Temple in Gansu was built in 1098 under the Western Xia dynasty, and it's the largest, best-preserved Western Xia royal temple still standing. The clay Buddha runs 34.5m head to foot; one ear alone is taller than a person. After the temple, the reward on this leg is a bowl of hand-pulled Gansu noodles. Expert tip: photos are banned inside — stand at the feet to feel the scale.
