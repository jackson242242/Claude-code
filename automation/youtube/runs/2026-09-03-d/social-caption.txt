China Travel Expert 🧭 | A giant sleeping baby lies alone in China's Gobi Desert

Far west along the old Silk Road, past Dunhuang, the empty Gobi hides "Son of the Earth" (大地之子) — a red-sandstone sculpture by Tsinghua artist Dong Shubing, finished 2016. About 15m long, carved block by block, a giant infant asleep on the gravel with nothing around for miles.

Tip: it's near Guazhou, Gansu — pair it with the Mogao Caves.
