## 3. Read-across to plans/longform-v2.md §5 (L01–L04) + recommendations
**Data supports the schedule's premise, with two adjustments.**
1. **Length:** the benchmark's own norm is ~25min (76% >20min), while our bible
   sets 11–13min and L01 shipped at 13.2min. KEEP 11–15min for the search-intent trio (visa /
   mistakes / payments) — long-tail search viewers don't need 25min — but once L01–L04 retention
   data lands, TEST one 18–22min episode to see if watch-time (not just CTR) rewards length the way
   it does for the benchmark.
2. **Topic ceiling vs. floor:** L01 visa / L03 payments / L04 $50-a-day are **search-intent floor
   plays** — modest ceilings, high buyer-intent (exactly growth-v2's "premium audience is in search"
   thesis). They are correctly chosen for the private-domain funnel, NOT for viral reach.
   To ALSO chase the viral ceiling, the awe angle over-performs 100x. RECOMMEND: insert one
   **"China Surprised Me / What Nobody Tells You About Modern China"** awe-documentary as L05
   (honest, fact-checked, no clickbait — the moat stays), alongside the practical trio. Do NOT drop
   the practical episodes; add the awe lane as a second track.
3. **Title discipline:** adopt the benchmark's numbered + power-word + second-person formula for our
   list episodes (L02 "15 Mistakes…" already matches). L01 could A/B a number-front variant
   ("7 China Visa-Free Rules Nobody Explains (Don't Miss #4)") vs. the current title.
4. **Keep the red lines:** the highest-viral China content trends toward "China terrifies America"
   geopolitical framing — we DO NOT go there (contentRules + bible §5 red lines). Awe = engineering,
   scale, daily-life wonder, never political comparison.
