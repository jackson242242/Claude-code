## 1. Forbidden City '9,999 and a half rooms' is a legend

- **Type:** Palace
- **Site:** Forbidden City (Palace Museum), Beijing
- **Hook (MISCONCEPTION):** Forbidden City's '9,999 and a half rooms'? Surveys count 8,886.
- **Claim / detail:** The 9,999.5-room figure is folklore. Rooms are counted in jian (the space between four pillars); experts' count is 8,886 bays in about 980 surviving buildings. A 2012 Palace Museum count led by Shan Jixiang reportedly found 1,200 buildings and 9,371 rooms (single source), and UNESCO loosely says 'nearly 10,000 rooms'. Safe on-screen wording: 'fewer than 9,999 by every survey; the exact count depends on how you define a room'. Built 1406-1420.
- **Confidence:** medium (myth busted: high; exact count depends on definition)
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_City
  - https://www.ourchinastory.com/en/amp/16029
  - https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/439/

- **Site:** Hall of Supreme Harmony, Forbidden City, Beijing

## 5. The Forbidden City's biggest hall dates from 1695, not 1420
