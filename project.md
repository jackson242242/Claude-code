## Release Notes

### New — 2026-06-10

**VoiceMemoBot: Voice Memo → AI Remix → Social Feed** (PR #23)
Record a voice memo from your browser or Apple Watch, remix it into music with AI using style presets, one-click tweaks (speed, echo, pitch, reverb), and instrument layers, then post it to VoiceMemoBot's own in-app social feed where others can browse and like your creations. No external social accounts required. A live web prototype is available at the deployed API URL — no watch or Xcode needed to try it.
