## Release Notes

### 2026-06-10

**New — VoiceMemoBot: Voice Memo → AI Remix → Social Feed**

Record a voice memo on your Apple Watch or browser, and VoiceMemoBot remixes it into music using AI. Tweak the result with one-click tools — style chips, speed, echo, volume, instrument layers, and a free-text sound prompt — then post it to the in-app social feed where others can play back and like your creations. A live web prototype is available at the deployed URL (no Watch or Xcode required to try it).
